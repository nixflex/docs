## Choose your task

| Task | Where to start |
| --- | --- |
| Support an incoming caller | [Answer calls](/guides/inbound-support) |
| Make an authorised outgoing call | [Call one number](/guides/business/call-one-number) |
| Review a finished call | [Call History](/guides/business/call-history) |
| Listen or download when available | [Recordings](/guides/business/call-history/recordings) |
| Check what the call booked or changed | [Booking results](/guides/business/booking-results) |
| Listen or take over an eligible call | [Live monitoring](/guides/live-monitoring) |

## Check the outcome

Use the task’s permissions and prerequisites before making changes. Read the saved result and keep an unavailable or uncertain result distinct from confirmed success. The linked guides explain the relevant recovery steps.
