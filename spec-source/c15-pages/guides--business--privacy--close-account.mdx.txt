## Before you start

Use the Owner's own login. Admins and Members cannot close the organisation. If you only want to leave a team, use [Leave organisation](/guides/business/team-leave). If another person should take over, [transfer ownership](/guides/business/team-ownership) instead.

Check scheduled work, connected calls, your remaining credit and the phone numbers that route to Nixflex. Closure stops scheduled calls and campaigns; already-connected calls may finish. Repoint your carrier routing to the replacement service before relying on it. Closing Nixflex is not the same as releasing a number from your carrier account.

## Download records first

In **Settings → Data and privacy**, choose **Download my data**. The download is one archive with Excel and JSON data. It excludes secrets and audio files. Download recordings individually from **Call History**, and invoices or statements from **Billing → Documents**.

The account-data download permits one request every 15 minutes, up to 100,000 records or 50 MiB, with a ten-minute preparation limit. Keep the file private and check it opens before closing the account. The download does not delete anything.

## Request closure

1. Open **Settings** as Owner and find **Danger zone → Delete account**.
2. Complete the fresh sign-in check when prompted.
3. Read the displayed balance and deletion scope. Remaining credit is not refunded; free credit is removed.
4. Type **DELETE** and confirm only when the organisation is ready to stop.
5. Read the result and account-status page. A locked or pending state means cleanup has not yet been confirmed complete.

## What is removed or kept

| Area | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Organisation work | Its API key, agents, number connections, campaigns, messages, call content and saved connections are included in tracked cleanup. |
| Nixflex recordings | Cleanup tracks the recordings held by Nixflex. |
| Personal logins | Team members' personal login identities remain after organisation closure. |
| Billing and trial protection | Required financial records and the free-credit protection fingerprint are retained. |
| Your own bucket and downloads | Nixflex does not remove your copies; apply your own lifecycle. |
| Carrier account | Your telephone account and number ownership are separate. Check and change routing there. |

## If the result is unclear

| Message | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Sign in again; nothing requested | Complete the fresh sign-in check, then review the confirmation again. |
| Balance could not be checked | Retry the balance read; closure is unavailable until the check succeeds. |
| Account locked, deletion pending | Keep the status. Do not describe it as completed erasure or create replacement work on that account. |
| Deletion could not be confirmed | It may already be locked. Contact support@nixflex.com with the account context, without credentials. |

Do not assume a fixed completion time when the status still reports outstanding work. Single-call deletion has a different resume contract from account closure.
