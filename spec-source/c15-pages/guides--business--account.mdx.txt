## Profile and email preferences

In **Settings → Profile**, use **Edit** beside the account name, then **Save**. Personal profile details and the organisation name are separate; use [Team](/guides/business/team) for organisation management.

Owner and Admin can review **Settings → Email notifications**. Payment receipts and low-balance warnings have their own choices; a low-balance warning needs a saved amount. Auto-recharge failure and empty-balance/service-paused notices stay on. Saving a preference does not prove that a particular email reached an inbox.

## Check the outcome

Use the task’s permissions and prerequisites before making changes. Read the saved result and keep an unavailable or uncertain result distinct from confirmed success. The linked guides explain the relevant recovery steps.
