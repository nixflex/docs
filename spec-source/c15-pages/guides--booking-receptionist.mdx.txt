Help callers book appointments during a phone conversation. The calendar connection supplies live availability and booking results. Your instructions explain when to use it, which details to collect and what to do if booking cannot be confirmed.

<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- Connect the calendar to the intended number. A prompt alone cannot enable booking.
- For a voice booking, collect the spelled name and email, then get one yes after the combined read-back.
- The calendar result confirms the appointment. A transcript or spoken promise does not.
- An invitation or text request is separate from inbox or handset delivery.

## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login and an agent with a connected number.
- Have access to the intended Cal.com or Google Calendar account.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Select the number">

<span id="1-select-the-number" />

Open **Integrations** and choose the agent and business number. Calendar setup belongs to that number. Check the selection carefully if you manage several locations or use one agent for multiple numbers.

</Step>

<Step title="Connect the calendar">

<span id="2-connect-the-calendar" />

Open **Cal.com** or **Google Calendar** and complete its connection. For Cal.com, supply the requested account and event details. For Google Calendar, use **Connect Google Calendar** and approve the intended account.

</Step>

<Step title="Describe the booking task">

<span id="3-describe-the-booking-task" />

Tell the agent which appointment the caller can book, what information to confirm and what to say if there is no suitable time. Put detailed services and conditions in knowledge. Use your real calendar time zone, without assuming the caller shares it.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose confirmation settings">

<span id="4-choose-confirmation-settings" />

For a new voice booking, ask the caller to spell their full name, first and last, letter by letter. Then ask them to spell the email part before @ letter by letter and say the domain as words, such as “at gmail dot com”.

Read the booking, name and email back together once. Wait for one clear yes before booking. This applies to every number even when the old optional final-confirmation setting is off.

If something is wrong, ask only for that part again. If the caller only says no, ask which part is wrong. A yes about one unclear letter does not approve the booking. Say it is booked only after the calendar confirms success.

SMS booking keeps its separate name and email checks. Looking up, moving or cancelling an existing booking keeps its current email-approval flow without collecting a new name. After a successful booking, do not spell the address again. A notification request does not prove delivery to an inbox.

With Google Calendar, review **Email the caller a Google invite** on the number's calendar card. It starts on. After a confirmed booking and invitation request, the agent says “Google will email you an invite.” Updates and cancellations use matching wording. This is not an inbox-delivery promise. Cal.com handles its own emails. **In-Call SMS** is separate: the agent can say it texted the details only after a matching send is accepted; that is not proof of handset delivery.

</Step>

<Step title="Test a complete booking">

<span id="5-test-a-complete-booking" />

Call from a phone you control. Spell the full name and email local part, say the domain as words, and approve one combined read-back. Make a deliberate correction and check that only the wrong part is asked again. Verify the appointment in the calendar. Test an unavailable time, then a move or cancellation using the existing-booking flow. Remove the test appointment through your calendar.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

Review early bookings against the calendar. A spoken promise or generated summary is not the final booking record. If a booking cannot be confirmed, the agent should offer a clear follow-up instead of inventing a reservation.

Your developer can receive the confirmed changes and final booking result after a call; see [post-call booking results](/api-reference/call-bookings).

## Check the result

| Result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calendar connected | Setup is saved for this number. | Test a complete booking. |
| Contact detail corrected | The earlier approval no longer covers that field. | Let the agent repeat its check. |
| Booking confirmed | The connected calendar returned a successful result. | Check the appointment and any notification request separately. |
| Booking result unavailable | The outcome is not established. | Check the calendar before creating another appointment. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Is a prompt enough to enable booking?">

No. The number needs a working calendar connection.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does this work in website chat?">

Built-in website calendar actions are unavailable. Phone and SMS booking have separate channel rules.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can each branch use its own calendar?">

Yes, calendars are connected per number. Shared agent knowledge is still shared across those numbers.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will every booking send a text?">

No. In-Call SMS and message eligibility must allow it; check delivery separately.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
