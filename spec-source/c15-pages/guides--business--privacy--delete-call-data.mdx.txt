**The important part**

- An Owner or Admin can delete call and campaign content. A Member cannot.

## Choose what to remove

| Action | What changes | What remains |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delete one call | The running call is ended first, then its customer content is removed. Nixflex recording cleanup is tracked separately. | Required billing evidence, used charges and copies kept outside Nixflex. |
| Delete a voice campaign | Waiting work is cancelled, its running calls are ended, and the campaign and recipient list disappear. | Calls already made stay in Call History; used charges remain. |
| Delete an SMS campaign | Waiting work is cancelled and the campaign and recipient list disappear after any required hang-up. | Already-sent texts stay in message history and cannot be recalled. |
| Cancel a campaign | Future dispatch stops. | Active calls may finish; campaign records remain for review. |

Deleting a campaign does not also delete every call it made. Delete an individual call from Call History if its content should be removed too. Inbox **Archive** only changes inbox organisation; it is not deletion.

## Delete one call

1. Open **Call History** with an Owner or Admin login.
2. Open the intended call and check its number, time and result. Download any recording you are authorised to keep before deleting.
3. Choose **Delete** and read the confirmation. A running call will be hung up.
4. Confirm once and read the result. A lost connection to the dashboard is not a success message.
5. Refresh the list. If deletion is still queued or unconfirmed, use Delete again on that same call if needed; an existing request safely resumes.

## Delete a campaign

1. Open the voice or SMS campaign and confirm its name and recipient list.
2. Decide whether you want to stop future sending only or remove the campaign. Choose **Cancel** for the first task and **Delete** for the second.
3. Read the confirmation, including the effect on waiting work and running calls.
4. Confirm Delete and wait for the result. Check Call History or message history separately for work already sent.

## Read the result

| Result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deletion confirmed | The selected content is removed from its normal customer view. | Keep any required follow-up records outside the deleted view. |
| Waiting for hang-up | A running connection has not confirmed it ended. Deletion is queued and continues automatically. | Check the list later. Repeating Delete on the same item safely resumes it. |
| Recording cleanup queued | Call content is deleted, but removal of the Nixflex recording copy is not yet confirmed. | Allow tracked cleanup to continue; do not claim every audio copy is gone. |
| Result could not be confirmed | The request may already exist. | Check the list, then repeat Delete on the same item if needed. |
| Item not found | It is missing or unavailable to this account. | Check the selected account and identifier. |
| Permission denied | Your role cannot perform this action. | Ask the Owner or an Admin. |

## Recordings and outside copies

Nixflex tracks removal of its recording copy. Carrier cleanup is best effort and does not prevent confirmed call-content deletion. Your own recording bucket, downloaded files, exported data and copies sent to other tools remain under your control. An already-issued recording link may work until it expires or the stored object is removed.

## Account closure is separate

This task does not close the organisation, remove its phone numbers or erase billing records. Automatic retention and bulk call deletion have separate rules. Use [close your account](/guides/business/privacy/close-account) only when the whole organisation should close.
