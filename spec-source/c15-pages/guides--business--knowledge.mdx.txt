<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Knowledge belongs to the agent.** Every attached number shares it. Different branch answers need separate agents.
- Keep behaviour and everyday rules in the main instructions. Put detailed business facts in **Knowledge base**.
- **Review new topics before they go live.** Adding a source does not approve it.
- Knowledge supplies facts. It does not itself make bookings, send texts or give permission to act.

</div>

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose one agent">

<span id="1-choose-your-agent" />

Open **Knowledge base** and select the agent. Check its attached numbers so the same facts suit every caller.

</Step>

<Step title="Add and review facts">

<span id="2-add-a-source" />

<span id="3-review-the-topics" />

<span id="how-much-can-i-add" />

Use **Add knowledge**, or **Edit knowledge** then **Add more**. Follow [Add and review your business facts](/guides/business/knowledge/add-facts) to prepare and approve topics.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose where to use them">

<span id="what-happens-next" />

Open **Settings**. Incoming calls start enabled. Turn on the separate switches you need for outgoing calls and campaigns, website voice, website chat or SMS. Campaigns share the outgoing-call switch.

</Step>

<Step title="Test before relying on them">

<span id="4-test-real-questions" />

Use **Test** for customer questions, then make a separate call to check the whole agent. Follow [Test and fix your knowledge answers](/guides/business/knowledge/test-and-fix).

</Step>

</Steps>

## What to check next

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No live topics** | Nothing has been published for this agent. | Add facts and approve them before Test becomes available. |
| **Waiting review** | Proposed changes need approval. | Read the proposed topics; preparation is not publication. |
| **Live topics** | Approved facts are available. | Test normal questions and exceptions, then check the intended channel is on. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<span id="does-my-website-update-itself" style={{ scrollMarginTop: "8rem" }} />

<Accordion title="Does my website update itself?" id="5-keep-it-current">

No. In **Settings**, choose **I updated my website**, select saved pages to read again and review the changes.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are automatic keyterms?">

**From your knowledge** shows useful recognition hints in the agent’s **Keyterms** settings. They do not use your 20-term manual allowance. See [Help your agent recognise names](/guides/business/knowledge/keyterms).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can each number have different knowledge?">

Numbers attached to one agent share its knowledge and channel settings. Use separate agents when branches need different facts.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I keep a copy or delete the knowledge?">

**Download a copy** gives current live topic titles, text and settings as plain text. **Download for developers** gives a fuller JSON copy. Neither returns original uploads. Prepared downloads expire after one hour; click again for a current copy. **Delete knowledge** removes the whole base after confirmation.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
