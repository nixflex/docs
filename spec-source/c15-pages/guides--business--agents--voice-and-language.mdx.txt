## Choose and preview

Open the agent’s voice controls and review the available catalogue. Nixflex may show voices from Inworld and Cartesia; speech recognition can use Deepgram. Choose the voice that suits the conversation and preview a short sentence with a business name and ordinary punctuation.

Set the intended conversation language rather than assuming the voice’s name selects it. Where multilingual behaviour is available, test switching languages and mixed-language names. A number may have saved overrides, so test the number customers will actually use.

## Help with difficult names

Use **Keyterms** for unusual business names or terms people say often. New manual lists permit up to 20 entries, up to 50 characters each, within the combined request budget. Keep the spelling used in your real source facts and remove irrelevant terms. See [recognition terms](/guides/business/knowledge/keyterms) for the exact limits.

## Read the test outcome

| Symptom | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Name heard incorrectly | Check the transcript, spelling and relevant keyterms. |
| Voice sounds right in preview but not on the phone | Check the actual number’s settings and test a complete call. |
| Wrong conversation language | Review the agent language and separate SMS/Web instructions. |
| Preview fails | Read the displayed error and retry deliberately; do not assume the voice saved. |
