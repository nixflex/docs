Zapier and Make let you push your call data into thousands of other apps without writing code. After every call on a number, Nixflex sends the **full call data** to your Zapier or Make webhook — from there your scenario can create a CRM record, add a spreadsheet row, send a notification, or anything else.
## Setup
Connect under **Integrations → Zapier** (or **Make**) **→ Configure**:
1. Pick the agent and the phone number you want to set up.
2. Create the webhook trigger in your automation tool:
   - **Zapier:** add a **Catch Hook** trigger (Webhooks by Zapier) and copy its URL.
   - **Make:** add a **Custom Webhook** trigger and copy its URL.
3. Paste the webhook URL into the dashboard and click **Save**.
The URL must start with `https://`. You can disconnect any time from the same panel.
## What gets sent
Unlike Slack or GoHighLevel (which post a short summary), Zapier and Make receive the **full call data**, so your scenario has everything it needs:
- Call summary, sentiment, and outcome
- Caller number and call details
- Transcript and any extracted data
It fires **once per call, after it ends**, using the same reliable delivery as Webhooks: it **retries on failure** and **stops on a bad URL**.
## Per-number automation
Zapier and Make are connected **per phone number**, so each number can trigger its own scenario. Pick the number first, then paste that scenario's webhook URL.
## Same engine as Webhooks
Under the hood this is the same post-call delivery as the [Webhooks](/advanced/webhooks) feature — Zapier and Make are simply pre-labelled destinations for it. Use Webhooks directly if you want raw control; use Zapier or Make to chain into other apps with no code.
## Tips
- Test your Zap or scenario with one real call before relying on it.
- Map the fields you care about (summary, sentiment, caller number) inside your automation tool.
