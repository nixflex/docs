**FIXED · agent** Shared call mechanics and action permissions still apply when a number supplies business instructions.

**NOT OFFERED → do this instead · number** There is no separate fixed welcome_message per number. A dynamic opener is generated and can incorporate the known caller’s name. Keep a common fixed message on the agent, or use separate agents when different verbatim messages are essential.

**YOU CAN CHANGE · number** For a different greeting per business, keep Dynamic greeting on (the default) and put the opening line in that number’s custom_prompt. For example, “Start every call with: Thank you for calling Oak and Stone”.

**SCOPE · number** Put common rules in the agent system_prompt and business details in the dialled number’s custom_prompt. The number profile takes precedence on conflicting business details. It cannot grant new permissions.
