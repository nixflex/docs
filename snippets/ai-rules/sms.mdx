**FIXED · call** With incall_sms_enabled on the agent, an SMS-capable imported number with valid credentials can send during a call. Inbound uses the dialled business number as sender. Outbound uses the originating business number. The other party receives the text.

**NOT OFFERED → do this instead · number** SMS does not automatically inherit the inbound phone profile. Put this business’s SMS instructions in sms_prompt and configure automatic replies for its number.

**YOU CAN CHANGE · number** Choose sms_reply_enabled and sms_prompt for this number. Keep business-specific details separate when numbers share an agent.

**SCOPE · message** A single text, an automatic conversation reply and a call-triggered text are separate sends. Keep each message’s sender, receipt and consent together.
