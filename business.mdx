Get your first business number ready to answer customers. Start with one job, such as answering service questions. You can add booking and other tasks after that first call works.

## 1. Sign up

Create your login from the [Nixflex sign-in page](https://dashboard.nixflex.com/login). If you were invited to a team, use that invitation instead. Use a desktop for the dashboard. The Owner or an Admin can set up shared business work.

## 2. Choose your number

Have an active number you own and its carrier connection details ready. Choose a test line first where possible. Importing a number changes its call routing, so plan the handover if customers already call it.

## 3. Create and connect your agent

In **Agents**, choose **Create agent**. Give it a clear name, choose its language and voice, and explain your business, its job and when a person should help. Add detailed facts in [Knowledge base](/guides/business/knowledge), then review them before they go live.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **business-agent** — Agents, Create agent with name and voice selected. Use staging test data.</Note>

Open **Phone Numbers**, choose **Import number**, enter its connection details privately and select your new agent. Wait for a confirmed result, then check the inbound switch. See [phone number setup](/guides/business/phone-numbers) for moving a number or updating its carrier details.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **business-number** — Phone Numbers, connected test number with attached agent and inbound switch. Use staging test data.</Note>

## 4. Make a test call

Call from a phone you control. Ask a normal customer question, an unclear question and something your business does not offer. Check the answer and the result in **Call History**. For appointments, [connect a calendar](/guides/booking-receptionist) and check the actual event too.

## 5. Go live

Correct anything the test found, save it and call again. When you are satisfied, start directing customers to the number. Check credit and any extra channel settings first. Going live here means your tested number is ready for customers; it is not a separate button.
