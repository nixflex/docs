**The important part**

- **Start with a number you own** and its carrier connection details. Importing it changes call routing, so plan the handover.
- Give the agent a clear job and checked facts. Explain when a person should help.
- **Make a real test call** and read the result before relying on the agent.
- Check credit and the settings for any extra tasks, such as bookings or texts.

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Sign up or join your team">

<span id="1-sign-up" />

Create your login from the [Nixflex sign-in page](https://dashboard.nixflex.com/login). If someone invited you, use that invitation instead. The dashboard works on a computer or phone.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose your number">

<span id="2-choose-your-number" />

Have an active number you own and its private carrier details ready. Use a test line first where possible. [Connect your phone number](/guides/business/phone-numbers) explains the handover.

</Step>

<Step title="Create and connect your agent">

<span id="3-create-and-connect-your-agent" />

In **Agents**, choose **Create agent**. Set its name, language and voice. Explain your business, its job and when to ask for help. Put detailed facts in [Knowledge base](/guides/business/knowledge) and review them.

In **Phone Numbers**, choose **Import number**, enter the connection details privately and select the agent. Wait for confirmation, then check the inbound switch.

</Step>

<Step title="Make a test call">

<span id="4-make-a-test-call" />

Call from a phone you control. Ask an ordinary customer question, an unclear question and something you do not offer. Read the transcript and result in **Call History**. For appointments, [connect a calendar](/guides/business/google-calendar) and check the actual booking too.

</Step>

<Step title="Start answering customers">

<span id="5-go-live" />

Correct anything the test found, save it and call again. When the result is right and credit is ready, direct customers to the number. There is no separate **Go live** button.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What to check before customers call

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Number setup confirmed** | The number has a confirmed setup result. | Check the attached agent and that incoming calls are enabled. |
| **The agent gives a wrong answer** | The saved facts or instructions need attention. | Correct the fact or instruction, save it and make another test call. |
| **The call has ended** | The conversation has finished. | Read Call History; recordings and other results can finish processing separately. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Can I set this up on my phone?">

Yes. The dashboard uses compact layouts on a phone. See [Use the dashboard on a phone](/guides/business/using-on-phone) for filters, full call details and browser guidance.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a colleague help?">

Use separate logins and the right [team role](/guides/business/team). Owners and Admins can set up shared work; Members cannot edit it.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does adding facts turn on bookings or texts?">

No. Connect a calendar or enable the relevant message action separately, then test that task and its confirmed result.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I see recent changes?">

Read [What’s new](/guides/business/whats-new) for the latest guide to released changes.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
