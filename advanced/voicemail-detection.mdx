On an outbound call, Nixflex detects when an answering machine picks up instead of a person. By default the agent leaves a short message and ends the call. You control what it says — or whether it says anything — from your prompt.

Every detected call is marked `voicemail_detected` and your webhook fires as normal.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The AI reads the first thing it hears">
    A voicemail greeting mentions leaving a message, a beep or tone, "you have reached", "not available", or the equivalent in any language. The AI recognises it from the transcript, not from the audio, so it works in every supported language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It leaves a short message">
    The agent says who is calling and why, in one or two sentences, taken from your prompt. It waits for the greeting to finish first, so the message lands after the beep.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The call ends">
    No conversation, no waiting for an answer. The call is recorded as `voicemail_detected`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Detection is always on, free, and needs no setup.

## Control it from your prompt

The message is built from your prompt. There is no separate voicemail field — anything the agent should say on a machine goes in the prompt like everything else.

**Leave a message with a callback number:**

```text
You are calling from Bella Hair Studio to remind the customer about their
appointment on Friday at 2pm.

If you reach a voicemail, ask them to call back on 020 8555 0199.
```

**Hang up without leaving anything:**

```text
If you reach a voicemail, do not leave any message. Reply with only
[VOICEMAIL] and nothing else.
```

The `[VOICEMAIL]` tag is what marks the call as voicemail. Keep it in, or the
call is recorded as a silence timeout instead and your campaign counts are wrong.

**Say nothing about the reason for the call:**

```text
If you reach a voicemail, only say who is calling and ask them to call us
back. Do not mention the account or the balance.
```

<Warning>
  The agent builds the voicemail message from your prompt, so anything in your
  prompt can end up spoken onto a machine that other people in the household can
  play back. If your call involves health, financial or other private details,
  say in your prompt what the agent may and may not mention on a voicemail.
</Warning>

If your prompt has no clear purpose, there is nothing to say and the agent ends the call silently.

## If your carrier's greeting is unusual

Detection works out of the box on normal answering machines in any language — you do not need to configure anything.

Some networks word things differently, and some business lines play a switchboard message before the voicemail. If you find calls on a particular network are not being detected, describe what the agent will hear:

```text
If the first thing you hear says the person is unavailable or asks you to
record a message after the tone, that is an answering machine.
```

## Carrier AMD — faster, but no message

AMD (Answering Machine Detection) is a carrier-level check that listens to the audio and reports human or machine within a few seconds, before the greeting finishes.

<Warning>
  **AMD ends the call — it never leaves a message.** If you turn AMD on, your
  agent hangs up around 4 seconds in and your voicemail message is never
  spoken, whatever your prompt says. AMD is for campaigns that want to skip
  machines cheaply, not for leaving messages.
</Warning>

- **Off by default.** Enable it per agent with the **Voicemail detection** toggle under **Call Controls**.
- **Twilio numbers only** today.
- **Speed:** around 4 seconds, versus around 10 for the built-in detection.
- **Cost:** roughly $0.0075 per answered call, billed on your own telephony account.
- When AMD ends the call, it happens before the agent speaks, so the call has **no transcript, sentiment, or summary**. This is expected.

If both are active, AMD wins — it is faster, and the call is over before the built-in detection reaches a decision.

## Which to use

| | Built-in | Carrier AMD |
|---|---|---|
| Default | Always on | Off (toggle) |
| Leaves your message | Yes | **No** |
| Speed | ~10 sec | ~4 sec |
| Cost | Free | ~$0.0075 / answered call |
| Languages | Any | Best in US / CA |
| Transcript saved | Yes | No |
| Carriers | All | Twilio only |
| Ended reason | `voicemail_detected` | `voicemail_detected` |

Leave AMD off unless you have a reason to want a fast hang-up. The built-in detection is free, works on every carrier and language, and is the only way to leave a message.

## On the call record

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `ended_reason` | `voicemail_detected` |
| `voicemail_detected` | `true` |
| `voicemail_detection_method` | `llm_transcript` or `twilio_amd` |

Use `voicemail_detection_method` to tell a free detection from a paid one when reviewing a campaign.
