## Account default and agent setting

Choose **90 / 30 / 7 / 1 day / Delete after each call**. The API represents the last
option as `0`. New agents inherit the account default, with a 90-day fallback.
Changing the account default does not rewrite existing agents. Duplicating an agent
does not override this new-agent inheritance with the original agent's preference.

`POST /v1/agents` rejects an explicit `call_retention_days` with
`retention_inherited`. After creation, `PUT /v1/agents/:id` accepts the setting.
GET/list expose it. `GET /v1/account/retention` returns the saved default,
`applies_to: "new_agents"` and `processing_enabled`.

## Eligibility is not proof of deletion

The worker checks the applicable policy and waits for required settlement,
post-call processing, recording writers and delivery decisions before erasing
eligible content. A cleanup or storage failure remains pending; it is not reported
as successful erasure. Read the reported `processing_enabled` state; a saved policy alone does not prove that cleanup is running.

`0` does not delete a live call or suppress its post-call analysis. Required
post-call data is prepared for the API/webhook workflow first. It does not promise
indefinite delivery retries or that an unreachable recipient received the data.
Do not rely on an expired call remaining available to an ordinary GET indefinitely.

Website messages capture the applicable agent policy at insertion. Zero-day messages
become eligible after the reply, on the next safe cleanup, without ending the chat.
Saved visitor cards and telephone caller memory are separate from call/message
retention. Disabling `remember_callers` does not erase either automatically.

## Recordings and copies

Automatic Nixflex retention cleanup handles tracked Nixflex storage, not the
customer's own bucket. Apply your own retention policy to BYO storage, downloaded
files, webhook receivers and other copies you control. Billing/finalization evidence
is not the same thing as retained conversation content.

For carrier recordings, collection/storage/cleanup may be pending or fail. Do not
assume that every carrier copy was already deleted or that an absent URL proves
there was never a recording. Independent carrier recording is outside a Nixflex toggle.

## Manual actions are separate

Switching recording off affects new work, not old audio. Number `true` still wins
over agent `false`; see [recording](/advanced/call-recording).
`DELETE /v1/agents/:id` switches the agent off; it does not erase its history.
Call deletion and account erasure have their own safety checks and pending outcomes.
A timeout or an HTTP request alone is not proof that all copies are gone.

This page does not promise that all service/provider data stays in one region or
certify regulatory compliance. Confirm your configured destinations, provider terms,
consent and retention obligations for your deployment.
