**Nixflex is a platform for building AI voice agents that make and receive real phone calls.** You write what the agent should do in plain English, connect a number from your own carrier, and it is live - answering calls, booking into calendars, transferring to people, sending texts, and writing up every call afterwards.

Build it in the dashboard with no code, or entirely over an API. Same engine either way.

## What does it cost?

Pay as you go from **$0.08 per minute** of voice. Calls you listen to live, and calls from a browser, are **$0.09**. Texts are billed by your own carrier at their rate. Switching features on costs nothing - you pay for the minutes you use. See [Pricing](/reference/pricing).
