## How the product fits together

An **agent** holds shared behaviour and knowledge. A **phone number** attaches to it and has its own channel switches and connections. **Knowledge** contains topics from text, files or selected website sources. A **campaign** coordinates reviewed outbound recipients.

Calls, SMS and website conversations have separate instructions and action permissions. Start with one clear task, test it and add channels when ready. [What is Nixflex?](/what-is-nixflex) explains the main parts; the [glossary](/guides/glossary) explains the words used throughout these docs.
