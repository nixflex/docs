Nixflex is an AI phone agent. It answers your business number, talks to the caller like a person would, and gets things done - booking an appointment, taking a message, passing the call to someone, sending a text. It can also make calls: reminders, follow-ups, confirmations.

## What is an AI phone agent?

An AI phone agent is software that answers a phone number and holds a spoken conversation, instead of playing a menu. It understands what the caller says, replies in a natural voice, and takes action - booking, transferring, texting. Callers speak normally; there is no "press 1 for opening hours".

## How does it work?

Your phone number stays with your own carrier and points at the Nixflex engine. When someone calls, their speech becomes text, the agent decides what to say and do, and a natural voice speaks the reply - the whole loop in about a second. You control the agent with instructions written in plain English. Everything else - interruptions, silence, languages, endings - is handled for you.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. In the dashboard you write the agent's instructions, connect your phone number and you are live - about five minutes, no code. Developers get the same features over an API, an SDK, a CLI or MCP. Both paths run the same engine; only the setup differs.

Pay as you go from **$0.08 per minute** of voice, billed by the second. Calls you listen to live and calls from a browser are **$0.09**. Text messages are billed by your own carrier at their own rate. There is no subscription and no charge for switching features on. New accounts start with free credit. See [Pricing](/reference/pricing).

## What makes it different?

- **Your carrier, your numbers.** Twilio or Telnyx, in your own account. Nixflex never resells or marks up numbers, and each of your customers can bring their own.
- **One number does calls and texts.** The same number answers the phone and replies to texts, from one set of instructions.
- **Listen to a live call.** Live Monitor streams a call in progress to your phone or tablet.
- **The agent knows the caller.** Name, email and reference from previous calls are in front of it before the first word.
- **One agent, many businesses.** A single agent answers hundreds of numbers, each with its own details, voice and calendar.
- **No flow charts.** You describe what should happen in each situation in plain English instead of dragging boxes.

## What languages does it speak?

Thirty, including English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Russian, Polish, Chinese, Korean and Turkish. An agent can be locked to one language, or set to follow the caller across the common ones. See [Languages](/concepts/languages).

## What does it not do?

It is not a call centre and not a phone system. It does not sell you phone numbers - you bring your own carrier. It does not replace a person on complex or sensitive calls; it passes those on. And it only knows what you tell it: give it your prices and hours, and it will never guess at the rest.
