**Nixflex is a platform for building AI voice agents that make and receive real phone calls.** You write what the agent should do in plain English, connect a phone number from your own carrier, and it is live - answering calls, booking into calendars, transferring to people, sending texts, and writing up every call afterwards.

Build it in the dashboard with no code, or entirely over an API. Same engine either way.

## Build

## What is an AI phone agent?

An AI phone agent is software that answers a phone number and holds a spoken conversation, instead of playing a menu. It understands what the caller says, replies in a natural voice, and takes action - booking, transferring, texting. Callers speak normally; there is no "press 1 for opening hours".

## How does a call actually work?

Your number points at the Nixflex engine. When someone calls, their speech becomes text, the agent decides what to say and do, and a natural voice speaks the reply - the whole loop in about a second, with the first audio in roughly half of one. Interruptions, silence, language switching and call endings are handled by the engine, so your instructions only cover your business.

## Do I need to know how to code?

No. In the dashboard you write the agent's instructions, connect your number and you are live in about five minutes. Developers get identical features over the API, SDKs, CLI and MCP. The only difference between the two paths is how you set things up.

## What does it not do?

Nixflex does not sell phone numbers - you bring your own carrier, which is also why you keep control of them. There is no visual flow builder, by design: scenarios are written as sentences. A knowledge base is not available yet, so facts go in the prompt or come from a custom function during the call. And an agent only knows what you tell it - give it your prices and hours, and it will take a message rather than guess at anything else.
