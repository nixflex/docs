Start with the task you want to complete, then follow its guide for setup and checks.

## Getting started

### Do I need to write code?

No. Use the dashboard and the [Business Get started guide](/business). Developers can use the [API quickstart](/quickstart). Both use the same organisation's work and balance.

### How long does setup take?

It depends on your number, business instructions and integrations. Create one agent, connect a test number and check real results before directing customers to it. A successful connection is not a complete call test.

### Can I use the dashboard on my phone?

Yes. The compact phone layout keeps the menu, forms and call details available. Call History has a **Filters** toggle and rows you tap for full details. If a link opens inside another app, follow the prompt to open it in Safari or Chrome. See [the phone guide](/guides/business/using-on-phone).

### Do I need my own phone number?

Yes. Keep the number in your own telephone account and connect it to Nixflex. Importing changes its routing, so plan the handover from an existing service. See [phone number setup](/guides/business/phone-numbers).

### Which carriers can I connect?

Twilio and Telnyx have supported setup paths. Use the instructions for your chosen carrier and number; do not assume every number has identical calling, messaging or verification requirements. See [phone number concepts](/concepts/phone-numbers).

## Agents and bookings

### Can an agent answer and make calls?

Yes, with the number's relevant settings, account permissions and eligibility checks. Start with [support calls](/guides/inbound-support); review [outbound calling](/concepts/outbound-calls) before contacting people.

### Can one number also reply to texts?

Yes, if it supports SMS and you set up the number's separate SMS instructions and auto-reply switch. Automatic replies are not enabled simply by connecting the number. See [SMS setup](/guides/business/sms-agent).

### Can it book appointments?

Connect the intended calendar to the business number first. For a new booking, the agent reads back the name and waits for yes, then checks the email with a separate yes. It spells the part before @; familiar domains are said as words and unusual domains are spelled too. Later messages omit the approved address. Confirm the actual calendar result. Google invitations and texts have separate settings and delivery limits. See [booking receptionist](/guides/booking-receptionist).

### Can visitors use the agent on my website?

Yes. The website agent can provide chat and, when enabled, browser voice. Its setup is tied to your business number, even though a visitor uses the browser. Built-in phone and calendar actions are not automatically website actions. See [website setup](/guides/voice-widget).

### Which languages can I use?

Choose a supported language and suitable voice for the customers you serve. Switching and voice availability depend on the selected configuration. Check [language settings](/concepts/languages) and test the actual conversation.

## Billing and data

### What does it cost?

See [standard pricing](/reference/pricing) and your account's current **Billing** information. Telephone charges and your own connected services are separate. An organisation shares its balance; Owner and Admin can see Nixflex amounts, while Member cannot.

### Will auto-top-up prevent every interruption?

No. Payments can fail or reach your configured cap. Review the balance and payment result, and follow up on low-credit or payment messages. See [billing guidance](/guides/business/billing).

### Is the carrier recording always deleted after import?

The pipeline requests carrier-copy deletion after storage and linking are confirmed. That does not prove all external copies are gone. For managed recordings, use authorised access to obtain a short-lived download link; a webhook's recording endpoint is not a public audio URL. See [recording storage](/reference/pricing#recording-storage-and-copies).

### Can I remove saved call data?

Use the supported retention and deletion controls. Some cleanup can remain pending, and required billing records or copies kept elsewhere have separate rules. Do not treat a deletion request as proof that every copy has already disappeared. See [data privacy](/guides/business/privacy).
