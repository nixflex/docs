Nixflex charges per minute of voice and per SMS message. No setup fees, no minimums, no surprise charges.

## Voice

| Plan | Per minute | Monthly | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pay-as-you-go | $0.08 | $0 | Testing, small apps |
| Starter | $0.06 | $99 | Production apps up to ~30k min/month |
| Scale | $0.05 | $299 | Growing apps, 30k–100k min/month |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | 100k+ min/month, dedicated support |

Voice is billed per second, rounded to the nearest second. A 1m23s call costs `83 × ($0.08 / 60) = $0.111`.

## What's included in every minute

- Speech-to-text (AssemblyAI / Deepgram)
- LLM reasoning (Claude Haiku 4.5)
- Text-to-speech (Inworld TTS Max)
- Real-time engine processing
- Call recording (stored 30 days, signed URL access)
- Post-call analysis (summary, sentiment, custom extraction)
- Webhooks
- Dashboard access

There are no separate per-feature charges. Everything is in the per-minute price.

## What's not included

You pay these directly to the underlying providers:

| Cost | Where it's billed |
|---|---|
| Phone numbers (Twilio) | Your Twilio account, ~$1/month per number |
| Voice minutes (Twilio) | Your Twilio account, ~$0.013/min for UK→UK |
| SMS messages (Twilio) | Your Twilio account, varies by destination |

Because you BYO Twilio, Nixflex doesn't see or mark up these costs. You get Twilio's wholesale rates.

## SMS

Nixflex doesn't charge for SMS. You pay Twilio directly at their standard rates:

| Region | Per message |
|---|---|
| UK domestic | ~£0.04 |
| US (after A2P 10DLC) | ~$0.0075 per segment |
| International | Varies by destination |

## How billing works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Top up credit">
    Add credit to your account from the dashboard. Minimum top-up $20.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calls and SMS deduct from credit">
    Each call deducts at the end. SMS deducts when sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Low balance alerts">
    Email when credit drops below your threshold (default $20).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto-top-up (optional)">
    Set a payment method to auto-top-up when balance falls below a threshold. Never run out mid-call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Usage tracking

Check current usage:

```bash
curl https://api.nixflex.com/v1/usage \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer KEY_ID:KEY_SECRET"
```

Returns total minutes, calls, SMS, and credit balance.

## Volume discounts

Above 100k minutes per month, custom pricing applies. Contact [sales@nixflex.com](mailto:sales@nixflex.com) for a quote.

## Refunds

If a call fails due to a Nixflex error (not Twilio, not your endpoint), we credit it back automatically. You'll see `refunded` entries in your usage logs.

For larger billing questions, email [support@nixflex.com](mailto:support@nixflex.com).
