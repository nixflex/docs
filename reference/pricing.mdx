Nixflex charges a flat **$0.08 per minute** of voice as the standard rate. No setup fees, no monthly minimums, no per-feature charges.

## Voice

| | Per minute |
|---|---|
| **Voice (all calls, inbound + outbound)** | **$0.08** |
| Inbound call on a [Live Monitor](/concepts/live-monitor)-enabled number | $0.09 |

Voice is billed by duration, rounded to the nearest hundredth of a minute. For example, a 1m 23s call is about 1.38 minutes, so at $0.08/min it costs roughly `1.38 x $0.08 = $0.11`.

<Note>
  $0.08/min is the standard rate. High-volume accounts can be set to a custom rate - contact [sales@nixflex.com](mailto:sales@nixflex.com).
</Note>

## What is included in every minute

- Real-time speech recognition
- AI reasoning
- Natural-sounding voice synthesis
- Call recording (stored on Nixflex; the Twilio copy is deleted automatically - see below)
- Post-call analysis (summary, sentiment, custom extraction)
- Webhook delivery with automatic retries
- Dashboard access and logs

Everything above is included in the standard per-minute price. The only optional add-on is [Live Monitor](/concepts/live-monitor), which adds $0.01/min on inbound calls for numbers where you enable it.

## Call recordings

When call recording is on, the engine saves the recording to Nixflex storage and then **deletes the copy on Twilio automatically**. This is deliberate: it avoids ongoing Twilio storage charges and means your call audio is not retained on Twilio.

You get your recording as a `recording_url` in the post-call webhook (and on the call record). To keep a recording long-term, **download it and store it in your own backend** - Nixflex hosts the file but does not keep it forever (see [Data retention](/concepts/data-retention)).
## What you pay Twilio directly

Because you bring your own Twilio, these costs go straight to your Twilio account - Nixflex never sees or marks them up:

| Cost | Where it is billed |
|---|---|
| Phone numbers | Your Twilio account |
| Carrier voice minutes | Your Twilio account |
| SMS messages | Your Twilio account |

## SMS

Nixflex does not charge a separate per-SMS fee. SMS carrier costs are paid to your own Twilio account at their standard rates for your region.

## How billing works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Top up credit">
    Add credit to your account from the dashboard. The minimum top-up is $5.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Calls deduct from your credit">
    Each call's cost is deducted from your balance when the call ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Auto-top-up (optional)">
    Save a card and set an amount plus a threshold. When your balance falls below that threshold, Nixflex tops it up automatically so you never run out mid-call. You can also set a monthly cap on auto-top-ups.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Usage tracking

```bash
  -H "Authorization: Bearer KEY_ID:KEY_SECRET"
```
