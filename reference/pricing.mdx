Nixflex uses a shared credit balance. Check **Billing** for your account's applicable rates, balance and payment status. Standard rates are shown below; custom account pricing and eligible bring-your-own discounts can change what you pay.

## Standard usage rates

| Usage | Standard rate |
| --- | --- |
| Inbound or outbound phone calls | \$0.08 per minute |
| Phone calls with a Live Monitor listener actually attached | \$0.09 per minute |
| Website voice calls | \$0.09 per minute |
| Website chat | \$0.01 per message |

Voice duration is rounded to the nearest hundredth of a minute. A call lasting 1 minute 23 seconds is about 1.38 minutes, or roughly \$0.11 at the standard \$0.08 rate. Enabling Live Monitor alone does not add its listening premium; a listener must attach to the call.

There are no setup fees or monthly minimums. The standard voice rate includes speech recognition, reasoning, voice generation, post-call analysis and supported recording and webhook processing. Features still need their own setup and eligibility checks.

## Your separate telephone charges

Your telephone account bills you directly for number rental, telephone minutes and SMS transport. Nixflex does not mark up those charges or add a separate per-SMS fee. Your carrier's regional rates, message length and encoding can affect its bill. Your own model, voice or storage connections can also have separate charges.

## Recording storage and copies

When recording is enabled and the carrier produces the audio, Nixflex verifies and imports it into managed storage or your configured customer bucket. Only after storage and linking are confirmed does the pipeline **request deletion of the carrier copy**. A deletion request is not proof that every external copy is gone. Do not assume Nixflex holds the only copy or that further carrier storage charges are impossible.

For managed recordings, call lists and webhooks provide recording status and an authenticated recording endpoint. The `recording_url` field is not a public MP3 download link. An authorised recording request can return a short-lived signed link when audio is available. Customer-bucket recordings use your own storage access; the managed endpoint does not return a download URL for them.

Retention limits apply. If you need a longer-lived copy, download it through an authorised flow and keep it in your own storage. Copies you retain, customer buckets and external carrier records have their own cleanup responsibilities. See [recording access](/guides/business/call-history) and [data retention](/concepts/data-retention).

## Credit and payments

Top up from the dashboard; the minimum top-up is \$5. Eligible work uses the shared balance. Owners and Admins can manage Billing; Members cannot see Nixflex money amounts.

Optional auto-top-up attempts a payment when the balance reaches your chosen threshold, subject to your saved settings and monthly cap. A declined payment or another failure can leave the balance low. It does not guarantee uninterrupted service. Check payment results instead of submitting another payment while the outcome is unknown.

A usage total, a payment receipt and a completed invoice are different records. Use the same period and time zone when comparing them. See [billing guidance](/guides/business/billing), [pricing basics](/guides/pricing-basics) or [usage through the API](/guides/developer/billing).
