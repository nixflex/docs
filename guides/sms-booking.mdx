Let customers ask about appointments by text and use a connected calendar to complete the booking. SMS instructions are separate from your phone-call prompt. Keep the conversation short, and check the calendar before treating an appointment as confirmed.

**The important part**

- Use the number’s SMS instructions; the phone prompt does not control replies.
- Confirm customer details before a new booking and repeat the relevant check after a correction.
- Knowledge explains the service. The connected calendar performs the booking.
- Check the event before repeating an uncertain request; a sent reply is not proof of handset delivery.

## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login and a number that supports SMS.
- Connect Cal.com or Google Calendar to that number.
- Have an owned test phone and a test appointment slot.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Check the calendar connection">

<span id="1-check-the-calendar-connection" />

Open **Integrations**, select the business number and confirm the intended calendar is connected. Check which appointment type and time zone it uses. A calendar attached to a different number is not automatically shared.

</Step>

<Step title="Write SMS instructions">

<span id="2-write-sms-instructions" />

Open **SMS agent**, select the number and choose **Set up agent** or **Edit agent**. Explain the booking task, required customer details and what to do when the calendar cannot confirm a result.

</Step>

<Step title="Add service information">

<span id="3-add-service-information" />

Add the main appointment facts to the SMS instructions. For detailed service information, use the attached agent’s knowledge and enable its SMS switch. Knowledge supplies facts; the connected calendar performs the appointment action.

</Step>

<Step title="Enable automatic replies">

<span id="4-enable-automatic-replies" />

Save the instructions and turn on auto-reply. State the business time zone where a time could be ambiguous. For a new booking, the agent asks for the name, reads it back and waits for yes. It then checks the email separately: the part before @ is spelled, familiar domains use words and unusual domains are spelled too. Saved addresses need the same check. Correcting one field repeats only its check. A single yes must not approve both fields; later booking messages do not repeat an approved address.

</Step>

<Step title="Test the booking result">

<span id="5-test-the-booking-result" />

Text from your test phone, ask for a slot and approve the name and spelled email separately. Check the new calendar event, then test a change or cancellation; those use email approval without collecting a new name. If confirmation cannot be checked, the agent must not claim a booking succeeded.

For Google Calendar, **Email the caller a Google invite** controls the email request and starts on. After the booking and request are confirmed, the reply says “Google will email you an invite.” Google controls delivery. Turning the switch off does not cancel the appointment. Cal.com handles its own emails. A reply must not claim an email or text was sent without the matching result.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

Customers can continue in the same SMS channel. If safe conversation state cannot be checked, automatic replies on a calendar-connected number may pause instead of guessing whether a contact detail was approved. Review incomplete requests and the actual calendar before a person follows up. Do not assume a reply marked sent was delivered to the handset.

## Check the result

| Result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auto-reply off | Incoming messages can be saved without an automatic answer. | Review the number’s SMS switch. |
| Conversation state cannot be checked | A safe contact approval may not be available. | Review the incomplete request and calendar before following up. |
| Booking confirmed | The calendar returned the appointment result. | Check the event and any invitation request. |
| Reply sent | The text progressed through sending. | Check delivery information separately. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does the phone prompt control these replies?">

No. Use the number’s SMS instructions.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can the website agent use the same booking tools?">

Built-in website booking is unavailable. Website custom functions are a separate, explicitly enabled feature.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a campaign teach the SMS agent its offer?">

No. Add the offer or reminder information before launching a campaign that expects replies.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is a booking summary enough proof?">

No. Confirm the actual calendar event or returned booking result.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
