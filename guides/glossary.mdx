Use these words when setting up your business or talking with your developer. The same business can use phone calls, text messages and a website agent, with different instructions for each channel.

| Word | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Organisation** | The shared business account, work and credit balance. Each person has a personal login. |
| **Owner** | The person with full organisation control, including Admin roles, the API key, ownership offers and closure. |
| **Admin** | A person who manages business work, billing and Activity, and invites or removes Members. Admins do not manage other Admins or the Owner. |
| **Member** | A person who reads shared business work and can listen to or take over eligible calls. Members cannot change shared settings and do not see Nixflex amounts. |
| **Agent** | The saved instructions, voice and behaviour used to answer or make conversations. An agent can have several phone numbers. |
| **Phone number** | A business line attached to one agent at a time. It has its own routing, channel switches and integrations. |
| **Knowledge** | Business information saved for one agent, organised into topics and sources. Attached numbers share it. |
| **Topic** | One saved piece of knowledge, such as a service description or cancellation policy. The dashboard may call it a piece. |
| **Source** | Pasted text, a file, a selected website page or a manually written piece that supplies knowledge. |
| **Draft** | Proposed knowledge waiting for review. A draft is not live knowledge. |
| **Campaign** | A saved outbound voice or SMS task with its purpose or message, recipients and sending rules. |
| **Channel** | Where a conversation happens: inbound phone, outbound phone, website voice, website chat or SMS. |
| **Inbox** | The saved SMS or website conversation list for a phone number. Archiving moves a thread out of the main list; it does not delete it. |
| **Keyterms** | Names or unusual words that help speech recognition. They are hints, not guaranteed transcriptions. |

A role describes what a person may do. It does not create a separate balance or a private set of business agents. The organisation’s shared API credential has its own authority and should stay with authorised server software.
