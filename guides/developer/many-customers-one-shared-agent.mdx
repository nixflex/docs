| [number](https://docs.nixflex.com/ai-rules.md#inbound-calls) | For a different greeting per business, keep Dynamic greeting on (the default) and put the opening line in that number’s custom_prompt. For example, “Start every call with: Thank you for calling Oak and Stone”. |
| [agent](https://docs.nixflex.com/ai-rules.md#agents-voices) | Language, general call timezone, pickup delay and fixed welcome_message belong to the agent. Numbers share those settings. |
| [number](https://docs.nixflex.com/ai-rules.md#knowledge) | A separate base or knowledge opt-out for one attached number is unavailable. Use separate agents for different knowledge bases or for a customer who needs no knowledge. Choose knowledge channels on each agent. |
| [number](https://docs.nixflex.com/ai-rules.md#phone-numbers) | Select a complete catalogue voice pair on the number or request inheritance with voice_pair_inherit. | [account](https://docs.nixflex.com/ai-rules.md#contact-controls) | An inbound STOP suppresses that recipient across the owning account, for both voice and SMS. The business number that received it is evidence only. |

## Two customers, one agent

Oak and Stone and Harbour Dental each have a business number. Assign both numbers to the same agent if they share knowledge, language and call settings. Map the signed-in customer to its business number on your server. Keep the credential there.

For a different greeting per business, keep Dynamic greeting on (the default) and put the opening line in that number’s custom_prompt. For example, “Start every call with: Thank you for calling Oak and Stone”. Give Harbour Dental its own opening instruction in its number profile. Configure each business’s SMS and website profiles separately. Outbound developer instructions do not inherit the agent’s inbound system_prompt or the number’s custom_prompt. Include the business and purpose in prompt or the recipient’s prompt_override. Fixed call rules and enabled knowledge and tools can still apply.

When Oak and Stone calls back a customer, resolve its saved number and send that value in `from_number`. On a verified booking event, match the business number to Oak and Stone before displaying the result. If Harbour Dental later needs private clinical knowledge, assign its number to a different agent. Agents are unlimited. There is no fee per agent. Customers pay only for usage (minutes and texts).
