This guide sets up live monitoring so your team can see calls in progress and listen in on the audio in real time — useful for support leads, QA, and anyone who needs eyes and ears on live calls.

## What you'll build

A monitoring setup where your team can:

- Listen to a live call as it happens, from a phone or tablet
- See which calls are active in the dashboard

<Note>
Live Monitor is listen-only today. Taking over a live call is planned for later.
</Note>

## Before you start

- A phone number attached to an agent
- Dashboard access for the people who will monitor

<Note>
Live Monitor is enabled per phone number. Numbers with Live Monitor enabled bill inbound minutes at \$0.09. See [Pricing](/reference/pricing) for the full breakdown.
</Note>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable Live Monitor on the number">
    In your dashboard, open the phone number you want to monitor and turn on Live Monitor. Once enabled, calls on that number appear live in the dashboard while they are happening.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch calls in progress">
    Open the monitor view in your dashboard. Active calls show up as they happen, so your team can see what is live at any moment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Listen in on a live call">
    From a call in progress, your team can listen to the audio live — hearing both the caller and the agent as the conversation unfolds. See [Listening to live calls](/concepts/live-listen) for how the live audio works.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's next

- Review [Live Monitor](/concepts/live-monitor) for the full behaviour and settings.
- Turn on [Call recording](/advanced/call-recording) to keep audio for review after the call.
- Use [Post-call analysis](/advanced/post-call-analysis) to summarise every call automatically.
