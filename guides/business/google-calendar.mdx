<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Each number has its own calendar connection and hours**, even when numbers share an agent.
- Use one booking connection per number: **Google Calendar or Cal.com**. Disconnect the other before switching.
- The agent checks the name and waits for yes, then checks the email and waits for a **separate yes**.
- Google is asked to send caller invitations when the switch is on. A saved appointment does not prove inbox delivery.

</div>

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the right number">

<span id="1-select-the-number" />

In **Integrations**, choose the intended agent and phone number. Use an Owner or Admin login and a calendar you can edit. Members can view the connection.

</Step>

<Step title="Connect Google">

<span id="2-connect-the-calendar" />

Choose **Google Calendar → Connect**, sign in to the intended Google account and approve calendar access. Return to Nixflex and check the account shown. Keep your Google password out of agent instructions.

</Step>

<Step title="Set hours and appointment length">

<span id="3-set-booking-hours" />

Check the calendar, time zone and weekly opening hours. Mark closed days as closed. Choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Closing time must follow opening time; an overnight interval is not supported.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose caller invitations">

<span id="4-choose-caller-invitations" />

Set **Email the caller a Google invite** and save. It starts on, including for an existing connection unless you turned it off. With it on, Google is asked to email an invitation, update or cancellation. With it off, the booking can still be saved without that email request.

</Step>

<Step title="Call to test it">

<span id="5-test-the-result" />

Book a test appointment, try an unavailable time and correct a name or email. Check the separate approvals, then move and cancel the test booking. Verify the actual calendar and any invitation separately. In **Call History**, open [Booking result](/guides/business/booking-results) to check the final appointment and changes recorded for that call.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Tip>
Running several branches? Connect each branch’s number to the calendar and hours that branch uses.
</Tip>

## What the status means

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The calendar connection check succeeded. | The connection check succeeded. Test the booking rules before relying on them. |
| **Not set up** | This number has no completed connection. | Choose Connect for this number. |
| **Reconnect needed** | Google access needs renewing. | Choose Reconnect and approve Google access again. Saved booking settings are kept. |
| **Could not check** | The status read did not finish. | Retry before relying on the calendar. This does not prove it disconnected. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="How is the email checked?">

The agent spells the part before @ once and says familiar domains as words, such as “s-a-r-a-h at gmail dot com”. An unusual domain is spelled too. Saved addresses still need approval. A correction repeats that check, and later booking messages omit the approved address.

</Accordion>

<span id="has-the-caller-definitely-received-the-invitation" />

<Accordion title="Does the caller always receive the invite?">

No. “Google will email you an invite” describes the confirmed request, not arrival in the inbox. Check the address and spam folder if needed. An email-domain check does not prove the mailbox exists.

</Accordion>

<span id="what-happens-when-i-disconnect" />

<Accordion title="What happens if I disconnect?">

Choose **Disconnect** and wait for confirmation. This number loses its calendar access in Nixflex; existing appointments remain and other numbers are unaffected. An action already sent to Google may finish, so check the calendar if a call was booking at that moment.

</Accordion>

<span id="does-this-send-a-text-or-enable-website-booking" />

<Accordion title="Can the agent text a confirmation too?">

Texting needs the separate **In-Call SMS** setting and a matching confirmed send result. The invite switch does not enable texts or owner notifications. Built-in website calendar actions are unavailable.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
