Connect Google Calendar so your phone agent can check and manage appointments for the selected number. Use one intended booking connection per number and test the result in the calendar itself.

## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login.
- Have access to the intended Google Calendar account and a connected Nixflex number.

## Steps

### 1. Choose the business number

Open **Integrations**, select the agent and the phone number that should book appointments. Check you chose the intended location; another number’s connection is separate.

### 2. Open Google Calendar

Open the **Google Calendar** card and choose **Connect Google Calendar**. Use the account that owns the intended business calendar, rather than a personal account selected accidentally by the browser.

### 3. Complete account approval

Follow the sign-in and calendar-access request, then return to Nixflex. Review the connected account shown on the card. Nixflex uses the granted connection; you do not paste your calendar password into the agent.

### 4. Check the booking rules

Check the actual calendar, time zone and availability behaviour with your team. This integration’s current availability search uses its built-in slot rules; do not assume all custom working-hour settings are imported from your calendar.

### 5. Test a booking

Call from an owned test phone and complete an appointment request. Check the event in the connected account’s calendar. Try an unavailable time and a change, and confirm the outcome before using the connection with customers.

## What happens next

Describe the booking task in the phone and SMS instructions you use. Knowledge can explain services and policies, but a live calendar action confirms the appointment.

## Common questions

### Does connecting send text confirmations?

Text confirmation also depends on In-Call SMS and recipient eligibility. Check actual delivery separately.

### Can I disconnect later?

Use **Disconnect** on the selected number’s calendar card. This removes the Nixflex connection; it does not delete appointments already in your calendar.

### Does this enable website booking?

No. Built-in website calendar actions are unavailable.
