Send call results to the business tools your team already uses. Most connections belong to a phone number, so different locations can use different destinations. A saved connection and a successful downstream action are separate things to check.

## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login.
- Have access to the intended destination account and a connected test number.

## Steps

### 1. Choose the number

Open **Integrations** and select the agent and business number. Confirm the location before changing a connection. Calendar and message tools can receive customer information, so choose an appropriate business account.

### 2. Choose the destination

Open the relevant card. Use **Slack** for a team summary, **GoHighLevel** for contact and note updates, or **Zapier** or **Make** for your own workflow. Calendar connections have separate booking guides.

### 3. Enter the connection details

For Slack, supply the destination’s incoming-webhook address. For GoHighLevel, supply its location and private integration token. For Zapier or Make, supply the workflow’s webhook address. Keep these private and use the fields shown on the card.

### 4. Save and check

Choose **Save**, then check the connection state. A test control checks only what its result describes. A reachable destination does not prove a complete workflow, correct permissions or successful record creation.

### 5. Test the final result

Make a short test call and check the actual Slack message, CRM note or automation run. Confirm the right business account received it. If the result is uncertain, check before manually repeating an action that could create duplicates.

## What happens next

Review the first real results and keep destination access current. Disconnecting a tool stops the saved connection from being used; it does not remove data already delivered to that tool.

## Common questions

### Does every tool get the full transcript?

No. Slack and GoHighLevel use summaries and call details; webhook-based workflows can receive more. Check the guide for the connection you choose.

### Can Make run during a call?

A configured in-call workflow can return a result during the conversation. Its response and permissions need separate testing.

### Can Members change connections?

No. Members can read shared work; Owners and Admins manage connections.

### Does this change knowledge?

No. Knowledge and external actions are separate. A connected action does not automatically import your business information.
