<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- The agent’s spoken claim is conversation evidence, not proof that the action completed.
- Settings enable an action; instructions explain when to use it and how to handle failure.
- A sending request accepted by the message service does not prove handset delivery.
- Test unavailable destinations and unsuccessful paths before relying on a fallback.

</div>

## Before you start

Use an approved test number and authorised receiving phone. Have Owner or Admin access for settings changes and someone available to answer a transfer test. Prepare the intended fallback for each action.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Match the instruction to its setting">

Review the agent’s transfer destination, In-Call SMS, post-call SMS and voicemail configuration as relevant. These have separate controls. Knowledge facts alone cannot enable an action.

</Step>

<Step title="Test a transfer with and without an answer">

Call from the test phone and ask for a person. Check the receiving number and, for a warm transfer, its briefing. Repeat with an unanswered destination so you can confirm what the caller hears and how your team receives any follow-up.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the actual text">

Trigger an approved message and inspect the recipient, content and delivery information. Check whether it was an in-call or post-call message. If the result is uncertain, read the saved message state before another send.

</Step>

<Step title="Check voicemail handling">

Verify **Leave a Voicemail Message** and the saved wording for the relevant setup. Listen to the actual voicemail. An earlier answering-machine decision can end a call before any message is spoken.

</Step>

<Step title="Review and improve the fallback">

Open **Call History** and compare the conversation with the recorded action outcome. Correct the handling rule if it promised more than the result established. For appointments, use **Booking result** and the calendar. Retest the corrected path before customer use.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer attempted, no person reached | The destination did not complete the handoff. | Check the fallback and real receiving number. |
| Text accepted or sent | Sending progressed, but delivery may remain unconfirmed. | Read delivery information before promising arrival. |
| No voicemail spoken | An earlier decision may have ended the call. | Review the call and number’s voicemail settings. |
| Action outcome unknown | The operation may have happened. | Check the destination and saved state before repeating it. |
| Booking mentioned | A conversation discussed an appointment. | Read the confirmed booking result and actual calendar. |
