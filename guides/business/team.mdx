Your organisation keeps the business's agents, phone numbers, conversations and credit together. Each person uses their own login. Give people the role they need instead of sharing a password.

## Start with the right access

The **Owner** controls Admin access, the organisation API key, ownership and closure. **Admins** run shared work and billing. **Members** read shared work and can listen to or take over eligible live calls, but cannot edit shared settings or see Nixflex charges.

## Open Team

Owners and Admins can open **Team** to see people, pending invitations and allowed actions. The Owner sets the business name used in invitations and notices. Members can respond to an ownership offer addressed to them without receiving team-management access.

**Last active** means the latest recorded action in this organisation. It does not show whether a person is online. **Not yet recorded** means there is no recorded action to display.

## Review shared work

Owners and Admins can use **Activity** to see completed actions and results. Use the Person and date filters to narrow the list. The Team and Activity views refresh when opened, when the window regains focus and after your own team action.

## Common questions

### Are team seats charged?

There is no seat fee. The default limits are 50 people including unexpired pending invitations and 20 new or resent invitations per UTC day. Contact support if your business needs higher limits.

### Does each person get separate credit?

No. The organisation shares its balance and work. Joining through an invitation does not create another business account or trial balance.

### Does removing a login revoke a separately shared key?

No. Only the Owner sees the organisation API key. If it was shared separately, the Owner must rotate it separately when access should end.
