Let your team work in the same organisation with separate logins. Agents, phone numbers, campaigns and credit stay with the business. Choose each person’s role before inviting them, so they receive the access needed for their work.

## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login.
- Have the person’s email address and intended role ready.

## Steps

### 1. Open Team

Open **Team**. The Owner can set the business name used in invitations. Check the people and pending invitations already listed before adding someone again.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **team-members** — Team, Owner view with people, roles and one pending invitation. Use staging test data.</Note>

### 2. Choose Invite

Choose **Invite** and enter the person’s email. An Owner can invite an Admin or Member. An Admin can invite Members. Members cannot change shared work; they can read it and listen to or take over eligible live calls.

### 3. Send the invitation

Send it once. The recipient opens the link and joins using the invited email. A new person creates a login; an existing login signs in with its usual security checks. Opening the link alone does not join the team.

### 4. Check the result

Return to Team to check whether the person joined. Invitation links last 24 hours. **Resend** replaces the old link and **Revoke** cancels it. If sending was not confirmed, check with the person before resending.

### 5. Review access

Owners can change Member and Admin roles. Owners and Admins can use **Activity** and filter by person or dates. **Make owner** sends a personal ownership offer; the recipient must accept it on Team. The former Owner then becomes Admin.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **team-activity** — Team Activity, Person and date filters with a confirmed action. Use staging test data.</Note>

## What happens next

The person uses their own login while the business keeps one balance. Members see a dash instead of Nixflex amounts. Removing a person keeps shared work and their personal login.

## Common questions

### Are seats charged?

There is no seat fee. The default safety limit is 50 people including unexpired invitations, with 20 new or resent invitations per UTC day.

### Can someone with their own account join?

A login already tied to its own Nixflex account cannot join by moving that account’s work or credit. Follow the invitation message or contact support.

### Does removal stop an active takeover?

New access ends. A person already committed to a human takeover may finish that one call. The AI does not return. The Owner or an Admin can end the call.

### Does removing someone change the shared key?

No. If the Owner separately shared the organisation credential, the Owner must rotate it separately.
