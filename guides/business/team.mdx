Give each colleague their own login to your [organisation](/guides/glossary), with a role that fits their work.

<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Admins can manage billing and see charges.** Give that access only to people who need it.
- Members can read shared work and help with eligible live calls. They cannot edit shared settings, launch calls or see Nixflex amounts.
- **The Owner controls Admin access, the organisation API key and ownership.**
- Use invitations and separate logins. Joining a team does not create another business account or trial balance.

</div>

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Compare the roles">

Read [Choose team roles](/guides/business/team-roles). The Owner can manage Admins and Members; Admins can manage Members. Only the Owner can transfer ownership or close the organisation.

</Step>

<Step title="Open Team and check existing access">

<span id="open-team" />

Owners and Admins can open **Team** to see people, pending invitations and allowed actions. The Owner sets the business name used in invitations and notices.

</Step>

<Step title="Invite the right person">

Choose **Invite**, use the email the person will join with and select the allowed role. Follow [Invite people](/guides/business/team-invitations). The recipient must join through the invitation; opening the email alone does not accept it.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the joined role">

Check the person’s role in **Team** after they join. Review access when responsibilities change. Follow [Change a role](/guides/business/team-change-role) or [Leave or remove a member](/guides/business/team-leave) when needed.

</Step>

<Step title="Review recorded activity">

<span id="review-shared-work" />

Owners and Admins can open **Activity** and use the Person and date filters. [Activity and CSV](/guides/business/team-activity) explains the record and download.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What the team record means

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pending invitation** | The invitation has not been accepted. | Check its state before resending. |
| **Joined person and role** | The person is now on the team with the displayed role. | Check that the role gives the access they need. |
| **Last active** | The latest recorded action in this organisation, not an online indicator. | Use Activity to review recorded changes; contact the person to check availability. |
| **Not yet recorded** | No action time is available. | Check the person’s role for access; a missing activity time does not show whether they can sign in. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<span id="are-there-team-limits-or-seat-fees" style={{ scrollMarginTop: "8rem" }} />

<Accordion title="Are there team limits or seat fees?" id="are-team-seats-charged">

There is no seat fee. Default limits are 50 people including unexpired pending invitations, and 20 new or resent invitations per UTC day. Contact support if you need higher limits.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does each person get separate credit?">

No. The organisation shares its balance and work. Joining does not add a separate trial balance.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I hand over ownership?">

The Owner sends an [ownership offer](/guides/business/team-ownership) to an existing member, who can accept or decline. A Member can respond to their own offer without getting Team-management access first.

</Accordion>

<span id="does-removing-a-person-revoke-a-separately-shared-key" style={{ scrollMarginTop: "8rem" }} />

<Accordion title="Does removing a person revoke a separately shared key?" id="does-removing-a-login-revoke-a-separately-shared-key">

No. Only the Owner can see or rotate the organisation API key. If it was shared separately, rotate it separately when access should end.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
