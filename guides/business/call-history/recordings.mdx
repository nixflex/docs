**The important part**

- A call can complete normally without a saved recording.
- Play and Download make an access check; an old temporary link may no longer work.
- A customer-bucket recording uses your own authorised storage access.
- Missing audio does not prove failed calling or completed deletion, and downloaded copies remain your responsibility.

## Before you start

Sign in with permission to read the organisation’s shared calls. Find the correct call using its time, agent and business number. On a phone, tap the compact row to open the full record.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the call and read its recording state">

In **Call History**, select the call. Read any processing, unavailable, retained-data or own-storage message before pressing a control. The transcript, booking result and recording can have different readiness states.

</Step>

<Step title="Play available managed audio">

Choose **Play** and let the browser load audio. Use **Pause** or the recording-position control as needed. If playback cannot continue, press Play again to request a fresh link; sign in again if access expired.

</Step>

<Step title="Download through current access">

Choose **Download** when the recording is available. Keep the file private and store it under your business’s retention process. Do not treat a temporary link as a permanent public recording address.

</Step>

<Step title="Use your own storage when indicated">

If the record says **Stored in your bucket**, use the storage administrator’s authorised access. The ordinary managed-recording path does not supply that bucket’s download link. Check the actual upload result rather than assuming every recording reached the chosen bucket.

</Step>

<Step title="Investigate missing audio">

Read the status and review whether recording was enabled for that work. Audio may still be saving, unavailable, expired or held in your bucket. If the state cannot be checked, retry the read or contact support with the call reference; avoid changing unrelated settings.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recording still being saved | Audio processing is not finished. | Wait and check the call again. |
| Expired temporary link | The old access link is no longer valid. | Request a fresh link from the call if audio remains available. |
| Recording unavailable | The audio cannot currently be supplied. | Read the stated reason; the call may still have completed normally. |
| Stored in your bucket | The file uses your storage. | Use authorised bucket access and check its retention. |
| Access expired | Your session cannot load the recording. | Sign in again and reopen the call. |

A carrier copy, managed recording, downloaded file and customer bucket are separate copies. A request to remove one is not proof that every external copy is gone. Review your storage responsibilities alongside call retention.
