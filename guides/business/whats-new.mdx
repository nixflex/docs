This release makes bookings easier to check and everyday account work easier to manage. Follow the linked guides for setup and details.

## Clearer bookings and caller invitations

- **A calendar for each number.** Connect Google Calendar, choose that number's hours and appointment length, and control **Email the caller a Google invite**. [Set up Google Calendar](/guides/business/google-calendar).
- **Two separate contact checks.** The agent reads back the name and waits for yes, then spells the email's part before @ once and asks for another yes. Familiar domains are said as words; unusual ones are spelled too. Corrections get their own check. [Prepare your booking receptionist](/guides/booking-receptionist).
- **Honest confirmations.** After a confirmed booking and invitation request, “Google will email you an invite” explains what happens next. The agent does not claim inbox delivery or a text that was not sent.

## More control over your business

| Improvement | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| Carrier details | Update the existing number's private connection details while keeping its settings and history. This does not switch carriers. [Number guide](/guides/business/phone-numbers#update-carrier-details). |
| Knowledge keyterms | See **From your knowledge** hints in agent settings and add up to 20 words or phrases of your own. [Knowledge guide](/guides/business/knowledge#what-are-automatic-keyterms). |
| Team access | Work with Owner, Admin and Member roles, review ownership offers and follow changes in Activity. [Team guide](/guides/business/team). |
| Deletion feedback | Campaigns wait for final results and charges. Calls with no stored recording can finish deletion after final checks; missing audio alone is not proof. ## A clearer dashboard and emails

The [phone dashboard](/guides/business/using-on-phone) has compact spacing, call rows that open full details, a Filters toggle and guidance for leaving an in-app browser. Browser zoom stays available.

Account, billing and team emails share an updated Nixflex look, with clear headings and actions. **The design change needs no action from you.** Follow any payment or account notice as usual.
