## Clearer bookings and caller invitations

- **A calendar for each number.** Connect Google Calendar, choose that number's hours and appointment length, and control **Email the caller a Google invite**. [Set up Google Calendar](/guides/business/google-calendar).
- **One voice booking approval.** The caller spells the full name and email local part, says the domain as words, then approves one combined read-back. Correct only the wrong part. SMS and existing-booking changes keep their flow. [Prepare your booking receptionist](/guides/booking-receptionist).
- **Honest confirmations.** After a confirmed booking and invitation request, “Google will email you an invite” explains what happens next. The agent does not claim inbox delivery or a text that was not sent.

## A clearer dashboard and emails

The [phone dashboard](/guides/business/using-on-phone) has compact spacing, call rows that open full details, a Filters toggle and guidance for leaving an in-app browser. Browser zoom stays available.

Account, billing and team emails share an updated Nixflex look, with clear headings and actions. **The design change needs no action from you.** Follow any payment or account notice as usual.

## Delete calls and campaigns

Deleting one call hangs it up first if it is still running, then removes its content. An Owner or Admin can delete; a Member cannot. A confirmed content deletion can still have queued recording cleanup. Nixflex recording cleanup does not certify carrier erasure or remove customer-managed copies. Used charges and required financial records remain, with no refund.

Deleting a voice or SMS campaign cancels waiting work, hangs up its running calls, then removes the campaign and recipient list. Calls already made and texts already sent stay in history. Used charges stay in billing; there are no refunds. Owner and Admin can delete; Member cannot. Cancel alone stops future dispatch and lets active calls finish; Delete also requests their hang-up.

Read [delete call data](/guides/business/privacy/delete-call-data).
