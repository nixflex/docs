<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- A saved connection does not prove a booking happened, and a failed status read does not prove disconnection.
- Check both the call’s booking result and the actual calendar before creating another appointment.
- Keep no booking changes distinct from processing, deleted or unavailable details.
- An absent email does not prove the appointment is absent. Invitation requests and inbox delivery are separate.

</div>

## Before you start

Have the affected business number and call time. Use an Owner or Admin login for connection changes; a permitted team member can review shared call records. Keep customer contact details private.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Identify the exact number">

Open the call and note the business number used. In **Integrations**, select that same number. A connection on a different number does not supply this number’s calendar.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the connection state">

If **Not set up**, complete the intended connection. If **Reconnect needed**, approve Google access again. If **Could not check**, retry the read first. Do not disconnect a working calendar merely because a status request failed.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the time being offered">

Review the calendar, time zone, weekly hours, closed days and appointment length. Confirm the caller’s intended date and time zone. An unavailable slot needs a different available time, not a promise that the calendar will accept it.

</Step>

<Step title="Review contact approval and the result">

Read the conversation for the required name and email checks. Open **Booking result** and use **Refresh bookings** when it is processing or unavailable. Inspect the actual calendar before retrying a booking that may already have completed.

</Step>

<Step title="Test the corrected setup">

Change only the relevant setting, confirm it saved and make one controlled test. Verify the appointment, any move or cancellation, and the separate invitation request. Remove the test appointment afterwards.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connected, but no suitable slot | Access works; the booking rules or availability may exclude that time. | Review the correct calendar, time zone and hours. |
| Booking result ready | Retained changes from that call are available. | Read the final appointment and timeline, then compare the current calendar. |
| Processing or unavailable | The result is not currently established. | Refresh the read; do not assume no appointment was created. |
| Appointment exists, no email seen | Booking and inbox delivery differ. | Check the invitation setting, approved address and spam folder. |
