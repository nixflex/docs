Change the booking rules for one business number without changing other numbers connected to the same agent.

<div className="nxf-facts">
  <div><small>Who can change</small><strong>Owner, Admin</strong></div>
  <div><small>Scope</small><strong>Selected number</strong></div>
  <div><small>Appointment length</small><strong>15, 30, 45, 60 minutes</strong></div>
  <div><small>Invitations</small><strong>Separate from booking</strong></div>
</div>

<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- Choose the intended number before editing; its calendar settings are not shared by the whole agent.
- Closed days must be marked closed. Closing time must follow opening time; overnight intervals are not supported.
- Turning caller invitations off still allows bookings. It stops that invitation request, not the appointment.
- Disconnecting keeps existing appointments and cannot recall an action already sent to Google.

</div>

## Before you start

Use an Owner or Admin login. Have the intended number, calendar time zone and approved weekly hours ready. If access needs renewing, reconnect before relying on the saved rules.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the number’s calendar card">

In **Integrations**, select the agent and business number, then open **Google Calendar**. Check the connected account and calendar before editing.

</Step>

<Step title="Review the full week">

Check every day, including closed days, and the actual calendar time zone. Set the opening and closing times your team can honour. A caller may be in a different time zone.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the appointment length and invitation setting">

Choose 15, 30, 45 or 60 minutes. Review **Email the caller a Google invite**. It starts on unless you have turned it off; its saved choice controls requests for invitations, updates and cancellations. It does not enable In-Call SMS or owner notifications.

</Step>

<Step title="Save, read back and test">

Save and check the current card. If the result is unknown, refresh before making another change. Test a permitted slot and an unavailable time through your configured phone or SMS agent. Check the calendar and remove the test appointment through your normal process.

</Step>

<Step title="Disconnect only when you intend to stop future use">

Choose **Disconnect** on the selected number and wait for the confirmed result. Other numbers are unaffected. Check the calendar if a conversation was booking during the change, because an action already dispatched may finish. Disconnect the existing booking connection before switching between Google Calendar and Cal.com.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings saved | This number has a confirmed configuration. | Test the resulting availability and invitation choice. |
| Reconnect needed | Google access must be renewed. | Reconnect and approve access; saved booking settings are kept. |
| Could not check | The read did not establish the current state. | Retry the read before assuming the connection was removed. |
| Invite requested | Google was asked to send the notice. | Check the address and inbox separately; delivery is not guaranteed. |
