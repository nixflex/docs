Leaving ends your membership in the business. Removing someone ends theirs. Neither action deletes shared agents, numbers, credit or the person's personal login.

## Leave the team yourself

1. Sign in with the personal login you want to remove from the team.
2. If you are the Owner, first [transfer ownership](/guides/business/team-ownership) and wait for acceptance. An outstanding offer is not enough.
3. As an Admin or Member, open **Settings → Leave organisation**.
4. Read the confirmation and confirm that you want to leave.
5. Wait for the result. Leaving records an Activity entry and sends a notice. If you no longer belong to a team, ask its Owner for a new invitation to return.

## Remove someone else

Open **Team** and check the person's name and email before choosing their removal action. An Admin can remove Members. Only the Owner can manage Admins. Wait for the confirmed result and check the members list. Removing an unused invitation uses **Revoke** instead.

## Understand what access ends

| Situation | Result |
| --- | --- |
| New dashboard work, Listen or takeover requests | Access ends with membership. |
| A person already listening | Nixflex disconnects the session; an unconfirmed disconnect may take time to finish. |
| A person already committed to human takeover | They may finish that one call, with only Mute and Hang up available. The AI does not return. |
| A separately shared organisation API key | It remains valid until the Owner rotates it separately. |

The Owner or an Admin can end a held call in Monitor. Preparing to take over before removal does not allow the removed person to start a new takeover afterwards.

## Common questions

### Is leaving the same as deleting the organisation?

No. **Delete account** is an Owner action with a separate closure process. Leaving only removes membership. Do not choose closure when you mean to leave.

### Does a notice email prove the action completed?

Check the saved membership and Activity result. Email delivery can fail without reversing a completed removal or departure.

### Can the person join again?

They need a valid new invitation and must meet the joining requirements. Their old membership does not grant access.
