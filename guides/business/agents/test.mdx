## Choose the right test

| Test | Useful for | Check separately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice preview | Sound and pronunciation | Full phone-call behaviour and actions. |
| Knowledge Test | Reviewed facts and unanswered questions | Calling, SMS, transfers and bookings. |
| Browser test call | A conversation with the selected agent | The actual telephone number’s routing and carrier behaviour. |
| Controlled phone call | The number, voice, instructions and enabled call actions together | The resulting message, calendar appointment or human handoff. |

Use only a destination and participants who have agreed to the test. For changes that can book, send messages or transfer, choose a disposable test result and know how to clean it up.

## Test three conversations

1. Ask a normal question with a known answer.
2. Give an unclear name or correct one detail. Check that the agent asks a useful follow-up.
3. Ask something the business cannot provide. Check the fallback and human handoff.

For a new voice booking, spell the full name and email local part, say the domain as words, then give one yes after the combined read-back. Verify the appointment in the calendar. SMS and existing-booking changes keep their own flow.

## Listen and review

Use the tester’s supported Listen control with your own signed-in session. Microphone or playback permission errors need fixing in the browser. A closed or ended session must not be treated as ongoing audio.

If the agent ends or transfers the test, the test robot may stop and say why. That message does not prove the receiving person answered. Review Call History, the action result and the real destination. Correct one issue and repeat that part of the test.
