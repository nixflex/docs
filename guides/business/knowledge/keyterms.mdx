<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **From your knowledge** appears under **Keyterms** in agent settings. It is not a new Knowledge base switch.
- Automatic hints come from live facts and appear in importance order. **+N more** opens the rest.
- **Your words take priority** over competing automatic hints. Automatic terms use a separate allowance.
- Hints can help recognition. They do not guarantee correct hearing, change an old transcript or set pronunciation.

</div>

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the agent’s Keyterms">

Choose the agent in **Agents** and find **Keyterms**. Keep the spelling used in your real business information.

</Step>

<Step title="Review From your knowledge">

Read the chips and open **+N more** for the full list. These are automatic hints from live knowledge; an empty list can be correct when no distinctive terms were found.

</Step>

<Step title="Add your own words if needed">

Add up to 20 words or phrases, each no longer than 50 characters. The whole list must also fit the displayed size limit. Shorten or remove terms if the save message says it is too large.

</Step>

<Step title="Save and test a call">

Save your changes and call from a phone you control. Say the difficult name in a normal sentence and check what the agent heard. Correct the source spelling if the fact itself is wrong.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What to check next

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A manual word hides an automatic hint** | Your chosen word has priority over a competing automatic hint. | Check its spelling and keep only the manual words you need. |
| **The list is too large** | The manual list exceeds a current limit. | Follow the displayed size message. A 20-term list can still need shorter phrases. |
| **An older list remains after another edit** | An unchanged older list is being preserved. | Apply the current limits when you next change the list. |
| **A new hint has not appeared yet** | Automatic hints refresh in the background. | Allow preparation to finish and check again; do not upload the same facts again. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does the background refresh change my facts?">

No. Saved facts remain available while recognition hints are refreshed. Web addresses and links are filtered out of automatic hints.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Members change these words?">

Members can view the chips and open the list, but cannot edit the manual terms.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why does a correct hint still sound wrong?">

Recognition hints help with hearing. They are not a pronunciation setting, and support can vary with the selected language and voice setup. Test the real conversation rather than relying on a chip alone.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
