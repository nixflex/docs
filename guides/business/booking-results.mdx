<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **The card shows the result at the end of this call.** Later changes made in the calendar may differ.
- Read the final appointment first, then open **Booking timeline** to see its changes in order.
- **Processing or unavailable is not “no booking”.** Refresh or check the calendar before acting again.
- A transcript promise is not confirmation that an appointment was saved.

</div>

## What your caller hears

Illustrative excerpt after contact details were approved. Each agent confirmation follows a successful calendar action.

<div className="nxf-conversation">
  <p><strong>Your agent</strong><br />You’re booked for Saturday at 10:00.</p>
  <p><strong>Caller</strong><br />Could we move it to 11:30 instead?</p>
  <p><strong>Your agent</strong><br />Your appointment is now Saturday at 11:30.</p>
</div>

For this example, check that the final appointment says **11:30** and the timeline includes **Booked** followed by **Moved**. The conversation alone is not the result record.

## Check the result

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the call">

In **Call History**, find the call and open its details. From a single outbound call, **Open Call history** links to it when the call ID is known.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the final appointment">

Find **Booking result**. For each appointment shown, check **Confirmed** or **Cancelled**, the date, time zone, attendee and service when provided. A missing field means it was not provided; do not fill it from a guess.

</Step>

<Step title="Open the timeline">

Expand **Booking timeline**. Read **Booked**, **Moved** and **Cancelled** changes in the order shown, including their appointment times and when the changes happened.

</Step>

<Step title="Resolve an incomplete result">

Use **Refresh bookings** if the result is still processing or could not be loaded. If you need the current appointment rather than this call’s result, check the calendar itself before booking again.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What the message means

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirmed / Cancelled** | The appointment’s final recorded state for this call. | Review the time, time zone and attendee. Open the calendar if you need its current state. |
| **Still being processed** | The final result is not ready. | Wait and refresh before relying on the result. |
| **Booking results could not be loaded** | The result could not be read. | Refresh bookings or check the calendar before making another booking. |
| **No confirmed booking changes were recorded** | A completed read found no confirmed changes for this call. | Check the calendar for an existing appointment before creating one. |
| **Details are no longer available** | Retention removed access to the details; it did not cancel the appointment. | Check the calendar for the appointment’s current state. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does this show the calendar as it is now?">

No. It describes this call. Someone may have changed the appointment afterwards; open the calendar for its current state.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does an availability check appear as a booking?">

Checking a slot or looking up an appointment is not a confirmed booking change. The timeline records successful creates, moves and cancellations.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does a confirmed appointment prove an email arrived?">

No. The booking, the invitation request and inbox delivery are different results. Check caller details and the calendar invitation separately.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I book again when results are missing?">

Check the result and calendar first. Processing, unavailable or deleted details do not prove that the first booking failed.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
