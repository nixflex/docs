## Before you start

- Owners can invite Admins or Members. Admins can invite Members only.
- Ask for the email the person will use to join. A login already attached to its own Nixflex account cannot move that account into your team.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Check your team">

<span id="1-check-your-team" />

Open **Team**. The Owner should set the business name first so the invitation identifies the right business. Check the members and pending invitations before sending another.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the email and role">

<span id="2-enter-the-email-and-role" />

Choose **Invite**, enter the address and select the role. Nixflex checks the format and existing invitations; it does not prove the mailbox exists. An existing pending invitation offers **Resend** instead.

</Step>

<Step title="Send once and check the result">

<span id="3-send-once-and-check-the-result" />

**Invite sent** means the email service accepted the request, not that it arrived in the inbox. Ask the person to check spam. An explicit failure says the email could not be delivered. If the result is unknown, check with the person before resending.

</Step>

<Step title="Let the person join">

<span id="4-let-the-person-join" />

The link lasts **24 hours**. A new person enters their name and password and chooses **Join**. An existing login uses **Log in and join**, including its usual two-factor check. Opening the link alone does not accept it.

If the browser is signed in with another email, use **Sign out and continue**. If signup was interrupted, use the login already created and reopen the invitation; do not create another business account.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm membership">

<span id="5-confirm-membership" />

Check **Team** for the new person and their role. **Resend** replaces the previous link. **Revoke** invalidates an unused invitation. Neither action removes someone who has already joined.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the invitation result

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email request accepted | The service accepted the invitation email. | Check the joined state; ask the recipient to check spam if needed. |
| Invitation still pending | The invitation has not produced a joined member. | Check the address and recipient before using Resend. |
| Link expired | The 24-hour joining link can no longer be used. | Resend an invitation that you still intend to honour. |
| Result unconfirmed | You do not yet know whether the request completed. | Check Team and with the recipient before repeating it. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Why can I not send another invitation?">

The default limits are 50 people including unexpired invitations, and 20 new or resent invitations per UTC day. Also check whether the address is already a member or has a pending invitation. Support can review larger-team needs.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can joining bring over another account's balance?">

No. A login belongs to one organisation. Joining does not move another business's credit or work and does not grant new trial credit.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does an invitation email confirm delivery?">

No. Check the person's joined state in Team. Email acceptance and actual inbox delivery are different results.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
