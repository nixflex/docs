<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- Use the intended Cal.com account, username and event slug for the selected number.
- A blank saved-key field keeps the existing key; it does not mean no key is stored.
- Check Cal.com before repeating a booking whose result is uncertain.
- Cal.com handles its own emails. A missing email is not proof that the booking failed.

</div>

## Before you start

Have access to the intended Cal.com calendar and the affected call or SMS conversation. Use Owner or Admin access to edit the connection. Keep keys and customer details out of support screenshots.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Match the conversation to its number">

Find the business number used, then select it in **Integrations → Cal.com**. Shared agent knowledge does not share calendar connections between numbers.

</Step>

<Step title="Review the event settings">

Check the account, username, event slug and real calendar time zone. Confirm the event type exists and represents the service the customer requested. Do not replace a valid time zone with one copied from an example.

</Step>

<Step title="Handle the saved key deliberately">

Leave **Saved — leave blank to keep** empty for ordinary settings changes. Supply a new key only to replace it. After an unknown save, refresh the card before submitting another change.

</Step>

<Step title="Find what actually happened">

Inspect the calendar for the requested appointment. For a phone call, review its retained **Booking result** too. Check required contact approvals in the conversation. A transcript or SMS reply alone is not proof that Cal.com completed the action.

</Step>

<Step title="Test after the correction">

Make a controlled booking, an unavailable-time request and a correction. Verify the appointment in Cal.com, then remove the test booking. If a result remains uncertain, give support the affected number, time and redacted error rather than repeating the action.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Settings saved | The connection configuration was recorded. | Test the complete booking path. |
| Key field says Saved | The existing secret is stored but not displayed. | Leave blank unless replacing it. |
| Appointment found | Cal.com contains the appointment. | Review its time and attendee details instead of booking again. |
| Result uncertain | The action may have happened. | Check the calendar and retained result before another attempt. |
