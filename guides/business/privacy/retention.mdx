<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- Changing the account default does not rewrite retention choices already saved on existing agents.
- Call retention is not a universal rule for SMS, website messages, contact cards or caller memory.
- After-call cleanup still has required final work; a setting is not proof that every copy is erased.
- Your downloaded files and customer bucket need their own retention process.

</div>

## Before you start

Use an Owner or Admin login. Decide which records your business needs and identify each active agent, channel and external copy. Keep the decision separate from leaving a team or closing an organisation.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Set the account default">

Open **Settings** and review the call-data retention default. New agents inherit it. Save only the period your business has chosen.

</Step>

<Step title="Check every existing agent">

Open the agent’s **Keep call data for** setting. Available choices include 90, 30, 7 or 1 day, and deletion after each call. Review each agent instead of assuming the default updated it.

</Step>

<Step title="Understand the after-call choice">

After-call cleanup waits for required final processing and does not interrupt a live conversation. Read the actual data state when reviewing a call; missing audio alone does not prove all cleanup finished.

</Step>

<Step title="Review the other channels">

Check SMS and website message handling separately. Caller memory and website contact cards also have separate rules. Disabling future caller-memory use does not erase details already saved.

</Step>

<Step title="Review files outside Nixflex">

Check downloaded recordings, data exports, your own recording bucket and connected tools. Nixflex’s automatic retention does not remove files in your bucket. Keep authorised access and your own cleanup process current.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default saved | New agents inherit that choice. | Review existing agents individually. |
| Agent setting saved | That agent has the chosen retention policy. | Check the actual retained content and other channel policies separately. |
| Call content unavailable | There are several possible causes. | Read the stated status; do not infer completed erasure. |
| Files remain in your bucket | Those copies are under your control. | Apply your own storage and retention process. |
