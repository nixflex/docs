Use Billing to understand the credit available to your business and the work that used it. The organisation shares one balance. A usage report describes activity; payment documents describe money paid and are a separate record.

## Before you start

- Sign in as Owner or Admin; Members cannot manage billing or see Nixflex amounts.
- Have access to the business payment method if you plan to add credit.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Billing">

<span id="1-open-billing" />

Check the current balance and any payment or auto-recharge message. Confirm that you are in the intended organisation. Your telephone account’s number rental and message transport are billed separately from Nixflex credit.

</Step>

<Step title="Review usage">

<span id="2-review-usage" />

Choose the period you want to inspect and review the usage types. Open detailed rows where available. A missing or pending amount is not zero; some work needs final processing before its cost can be confirmed.

</Step>

<Step title="Add credit if needed">

<span id="3-add-credit-if-needed" />

Use the top-up control and complete the payment page. Return to Billing and wait for the recorded result. If the payment is still being checked, do not create another payment just because the browser did not finish its return.

</Step>

<Step title="Check automatic recharge">

<span id="4-check-automatic-recharge" />

Review **Auto-recharge**, the saved payment method and the selected thresholds. Save deliberate changes. If a card is declined, follow the displayed message and check the saved configuration before trying again.

</Step>

<Step title="Download the right report">

<span id="5-download-the-right-report" />

Use **Documents** for available payment records and the reporting controls for usage, statements or period exports. Choose the business’s actual reporting dates and time zone. Keep the currency shown on each document.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

Keep the balance sufficient for the channels you use. A channel being enabled does not prove that every future request has credit or capacity. Review failed payments and unresolved amounts in Billing.

## Read the billing result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recorded payment | Billing has a saved payment result. | Check the balance and the matching payment document. |
| Payment being checked | The final result is not confirmed. | Wait and review Billing before another payment. |
| Pending usage amount | The cost is not final. | Review again after processing; do not count it as zero. |
| Card declined | The payment was refused. | Follow the displayed message and check the saved payment method. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Who can see billing?">

Owners and Admins. Members see a dash instead of Nixflex amounts.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are calls charged when nobody speaks?">

A connected call can still be billable. Do not use transcript length as the measure of charged time.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are live listening and website calls priced differently?">

Actual feature use can affect the rate. Check current pricing and the recorded call result; enabling a switch alone is not a charge.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is a usage total an invoice?">

No. Usage can be pending or incomplete. Use payment documents for payment records.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why do Nixflex emails look different?">

Billing, account and team messages use the same updated Nixflex design, with clearer headings and actions. The design change needs no action from you and does not change your email preferences. Follow any payment or account notice as usual.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
