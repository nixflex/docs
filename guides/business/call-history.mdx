**The important part**

- **A summary or transcript is not proof that an action succeeded.** Check its recorded result.
- Booking details, recordings and summaries can finish processing separately.
- **Missing audio is not proof of deletion.** Read the recording state and wait for any deletion confirmation.
- Members can read shared calls but do not see Nixflex amounts.

## What to check after a conversation

Illustrative excerpt. Follow the claim into the Booking result card and calendar before treating it as confirmed.

<div className="nxf-conversation">
  <p><strong>Caller</strong><br />Can you move my appointment to Friday?</p>
  <p><strong>Your agent</strong><br />Your appointment has moved to Friday at 2:00.</p>
</div>

Open **Booking result** to check the final time and the **Moved** entry in its timeline. If the result is still processing or unavailable, the transcript alone does not settle it.

## Review a call

<Steps>

<Step title="Find and open the call">

<span id="1-find-the-call" />

Open **Call History** and choose the period and filters. Narrow by agent, business number, direction or outcome. On a phone, use **Filters** or **Hide filters** and tap the compact row for full details. [Phone guidance](/guides/business/using-on-phone) explains the layout.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the conversation">

<span id="2-read-the-conversation" />

Read the transcript and summary. **Knowledge available**, when present, shows topics supplied to the agent; it does not prove every fact was used correctly.

</Step>

<Step title="Check confirmed results">

<span id="3-check-the-outcome" />

Open **Booking result** for the final appointment and ordered changes from that call. Use **Refresh bookings** for processing or unavailable results. See [Check the booking result of a call](/guides/business/booking-results) for every state. Check transfer, message and other action results separately.

</Step>

<Step title="Play or download available audio">

<span id="4-play-or-download-audio" />

Choose **Play** or **Download** when available. Each request makes a fresh access check. Read whether the recording is processing, expired, unavailable or in your own storage.

</Step>

<Step title="Review cost and improve the agent">

<span id="5-review-cost-and-improvements" />

Owners and Admins can review confirmed Nixflex amounts, including after a human takeover. Correct an inaccurate fact or unclear instruction, then test it again. Keep a short list of recurring problems.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What a missing result means

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Processing** | The result is not finished yet. | Wait and refresh the relevant result. Other parts of the call may already be ready. |
| **Unavailable** | The result could not be read. | Check the stated reason and try the read again. Do not assume the action failed. |
| **Confirmed booking change** | The recorded action succeeded. | Check the final appointment and timeline; later calendar changes may differ. |
| **Expired download link** | The temporary recording link is no longer valid. | Request a fresh download if the recording remains available. |
| **Stored in your own bucket** | The audio is held in your storage. | Review your storage and its retention settings. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Why is the recording missing?">

It may be disabled, processing, expired, unavailable or stored in your own bucket. Read the stated status. A call can finish normally without a saved recording.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete a call just after it ends?">

Final processing and charges must finish first. If no recording was stored, deletion can finish once Nixflex has checked that no recording copy or upload remains. Pending checks keep it from completing. A missing Play button is not proof of deletion, and required billing records can remain.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is no transcript the same as no charge?">

No. A connected call can be billable without a conversation transcript.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do Members see costs?">

Members can read shared calls but do not see Nixflex amounts.

</Accordion>

<span id="does-deleting-an-agent-erase-these-calls" />

<Accordion title="Does switching off an agent erase its calls?">

Switching off an agent does not erase its history. Deletion and retention are separate actions. Retention can remove eligible content after required processing finishes; downloaded copies and your own storage need your own retention process.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
