Use Call History to review how your agent handled a conversation and what happened next. The record can include a transcript, summary, action results and recording state. These arrive through separate processing steps and may not all be ready together.

## Before you start

- Sign in with access to the organisation’s shared calls.
- Know the approximate date, business number or agent you want to review.

## Steps

### 1. Find the call

Open **Call History** and choose the relevant period and filters. Use the agent, business number, direction or outcome to narrow results. Incoming, outgoing, website and test calls can have different labels.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **call-history-list** — Call History, date and direction filters with test rows. Use staging test data.</Note>

### 2. Read the conversation

Open the call and review the transcript. Treat a summary as a helpful overview, not proof that every action succeeded. If knowledge evidence is present, **Knowledge available** shows the topics supplied to the agent, not a guarantee it used every fact.

### 3. Check the outcome

Look for confirmed booking, transfer or ending information. A request accepted for processing may still be unresolved. If a result is missing, check later or investigate the named action rather than assuming it failed or succeeded.

### 4. Play or download audio

Use **Play** or **Download** when a recording is available. A fresh access check is made. Pending, expired, unavailable and stored-in-your-bucket states mean different things. An expired download link can need a fresh request.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **call-history-recording** — Call details, recording available with transcript and summary. Use staging test data.</Note>

### 5. Review cost and improvements

Owners and Admins can review Nixflex amounts. Compare the call’s confirmed billing information with the work performed, especially after a human takeover. Update an incorrect knowledge topic or unclear instruction, then test the correction.

## What happens next

Keep a short list of recurring problems and correct their source. Retention can remove eligible call content after required processing finishes. Downloaded copies and files in your own storage need your own retention process.

## Common questions

### Why is the recording missing?

It may be disabled, pending, expired, unavailable or stored in your own bucket. Read the stated status.

### Is no transcript the same as no charge?

No. A connected call can be billable even without a conversation transcript.

### Do Members see costs?

Members can read shared calls but do not see Nixflex amounts.

### Does deleting an agent erase these calls?

Switching off an agent does not erase its history. Deletion and retention are separate actions.
