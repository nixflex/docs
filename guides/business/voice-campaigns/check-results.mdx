**The important part**

- A campaign status summarises work; individual calls explain what happened to each person.
- A missing response or uncertain result is not proof that no call started.
- Resume continues eligible unsent work. Use again prepares new work and needs a fresh review.
- Check bookings and messages separately from the conversation summary.

## Before you start

Choose the saved campaign by its name, date, agent and sending number. Use a permitted organisation login to review shared work; launching or changing campaigns requires Owner or Admin access.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the saved campaign">

In **Batch calls**, click the campaign row or focus it and press Enter. Opening the row shows its saved details. **Use again** starts a new setup, so do not use it just to read a result.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the campaign and recipient states">

Check the overall state, then each recipient’s result and waiting reason. Scheduled work still depends on recipient hours, restrictions and available capacity. Completed work does not mean every person answered.

</Step>

<Step title="Open calls that need review">

Use **Call History** to inspect the relevant call. Read the conversation alongside action results. If an appointment was discussed, open **Booking result** for the final appointment and ordered changes recorded during that call.

</Step>

<Step title="Resolve uncertainty before more work">

For **Needs checking**, keep the saved campaign and inspect current results. Do not launch a replacement list merely because a response was lost. If the result cannot be established, give support the campaign and call details without secrets.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose a deliberate next step">

Use **Resume** only when you intend to continue eligible unsent work. Review recipients and permission again before any new setup made with **Use again**. Use your team’s normal process for human follow-up based on the confirmed outcome.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled | A start is saved, but dispatch checks still apply. | Review timing and eligibility. |
| Running | Calls proceed as current checks and capacity allow. | Follow individual results. |
| Needs checking | A result is uncertain. | Read the saved state before launching more work. |
| Cancelled | Further calls will not be dispatched; started calls may finish. | Review the final call outcomes separately. |
| Completed | The campaign’s work finished. | Read the recipient outcomes; do not infer everyone answered. |

Campaign removal is a separate action from reviewing results. Read the current dashboard confirmation before any destructive action.
