- The campaign needs its full purpose; it does not automatically use the incoming-call instructions.

## Before you start

Use an Owner or Admin login. Choose the campaign’s active agent and outbound-enabled business number. Have the customer permission records, recipient details and approved call purpose available.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Prepare the purpose and recipient fields">

In **Batch calls → New campaign**, write who is calling, why, what to ask and when to finish. Check which personal details the purpose uses so every recipient has the required information.

</Step>

<Step title="Paste or upload the list">

Review the detected international numbers, repeated entries and personal details. Do not assume a successful file read means the contents are correct. Fix a missing required detail before launch.

</Step>

<Step title="Review contact restrictions">

Check held rows against the business do-not-contact list and locked recipient opt-outs. A business-added restriction and a recipient’s opt-out are different records. Removing one does not clear the other.

</Step>

<Step title="Review the schedule in recipient time">

Choose the start date, days and calling window. Review each recipient’s possible local time zones and provide a known country or time zone where needed. Your office clock is not a substitute for the recipient’s location.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm this exact version">

Correct the input, then review permission again. Use an explicit skip option only for the invalid rows it covers; it does not fill missing required details. Wider hours require an allowed choice and reason and still respect hard limits. Launch once only when the final list is ready.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ready | Current contact and timing checks allow the row. | Confirm your own permission record; checks run again before contact. |
| Waiting | The recipient’s current timing does not allow dispatch. | Review the permitted local window. |
| Location unknown | Nixflex cannot safely determine timing. | Provide the confirmed country or time zone and review again. |
| Restricted or opted out | A contact restriction applies. | Keep the row held; do not bypass a locked opt-out. |
| Check unavailable | Eligibility could not be established. | Wait for the check to recover before launch. |

If you edit recipients after reviewing them, repeat the review before confirming. A campaign’s scheduled start does not mean every recipient will be called at that moment; capacity and current checks still apply.
