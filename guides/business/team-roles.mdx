Choose a role by the work a person needs to do. The Owner keeps control of the business; Admins can run it, including its money; Members can read shared work and help with eligible live calls.

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Read agents, calls, recordings, transcripts and analytics | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Listen live and take over eligible calls | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create, edit or delete shared work and settings | Yes | Yes | No |
| View Nixflex amounts and manage billing, cards and auto-recharge | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite or remove Members and view Team Activity | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite, promote, demote or remove Admins | Yes | No | No |
| See or rotate the organisation API key | Yes | No | No |
| Offer ownership or close the organisation | Yes | No | No |

## What Members see

Members see a dash in place of Nixflex amounts, including call costs. This does not hide business prices in customer conversations. Disabled edit controls explain that an Admin or the Owner is needed. Members still manage their own login security and can leave the team.

## What Admins can do

Admins can see charges, add credit and manage shared business settings. Invite someone as an Admin only if they need that authority. Admins can manage Members but cannot give themselves or someone else Admin or Owner access.

## What stays with the Owner

There is one Owner. To hand over, send an [ownership offer](/guides/business/team-ownership) to an existing member and let them accept it. The previous Owner becomes Admin. A role dropdown alone does not transfer ownership.

## Common questions

### Can a Member accept ownership?

Yes, if the current Owner addresses an offer to them. Their personal offer banner does not open the rest of Team management or Activity.

### Do dashboard roles limit a separately shared API key?

No. The organisation key has its own authority. Do not give a Member that credential as a way to connect a tool with Member-only access.

### Does Member mean no access to customer information?

No. Members can read calls, transcripts and recordings. Choose membership carefully even when someone does not need billing access.
