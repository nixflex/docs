**The important part**

- Sent means waiting for delivery confirmation. Do not send it again just because delivery is pending.
- Read the selected recipient’s reason; waiting can reflect local hours or a delivery still in progress.
- The saved campaign text and the later SMS conversation are separate records.
- Cancelling future sending cannot recall messages already handed off.

## Before you start

Have the saved SMS campaign and the intended sending number. Use a permitted team login to read shared work. Confirm the date and recipient before deciding whether to follow up.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Delivery history">

Open the saved campaign and its **Delivery history**. Select the recipient you need. Use **Previous recipients** or **Next recipients** when the list has more pages.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the reason and saved message">

Check the delivery label, explanatory text and sent time when recorded. Read **Saved message** and **Estimated message parts**. Older records may not have a saved message or confirmed segment estimate; missing information is not a blank text or zero cost.

</Step>

<Step title="Separate waiting from failure">

A row may be waiting for the scheduled date, local hours or delivery confirmation. A held row can need permission or another check. Read the reason rather than guessing from colour alone.

</Step>

<Step title="Review replies in the correct inbox">

Open **SMS agent**, select the sending number and read its conversation. Automatic replies use that number’s saved SMS instructions and enabled knowledge; they do not learn the campaign text automatically.

</Step>

<Step title="Follow up only after checking the outcome">

For a failed or unclear result, read the available reason and contact support when detailed investigation is needed. Do not repeat an accepted or uncertain message. **Resume** continues eligible unsent work; it does not resend delivered texts.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivered | Delivery was confirmed, or a reply was received. | Review any reply in the SMS inbox. |
| Sent | Sending progressed; delivery confirmation is pending. | Wait and check; do not resend. |
| Waiting | The schedule, local hours or delivery progress is unresolved. | Read the selected row’s explanation. |
| Held | Permission, restrictions, cancellation or another check blocks progress. | Resolve only the stated issue; keep locked opt-outs blocked. |
| Failed | The message was not delivered. | Review the reason before a deliberate new contact attempt. |
