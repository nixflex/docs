**The important part**

- Preview each person’s finished message, not just the shared template.
- Missing personal details can block sending. Text length and encoding affect segment estimates.
- Changing the recipient input requires a fresh permission review.
- Prepare the sending number’s SMS agent before launching if you expect customer replies.

## Before you start

Use an Owner or Admin login and an active SMS-capable sending number. Have the approved message, recipient details and permission records ready. Keep the message specific to the task.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose the sending number and purpose">

Create the SMS campaign under **Outbound**. Check the agent and business number, and use a name your team can recognise later.

</Step>

<Step title="Write the message and supply its details">

Add the intended recipient details to the text. Paste or upload the list, then check detected numbers and duplicate rows. Correct missing values instead of assuming the template can fill them.

</Step>

<Step title="Inspect each finished message">

Open recipient previews and read the actual message. Check dates, times, links and the way to opt out where appropriate. Review estimated message parts; unusual characters or another language can change the transport cost.

</Step>

<Step title="Review restrictions and local timing">

Inspect invalid numbers, do-not-contact entries, locked opt-outs and waiting rows. Choose the schedule and allowed window. Supply the known country or time zone if location cannot be established; do not use your business clock as a fallback.

</Step>

<Step title="Prepare replies and confirm the final list">

Save the offer or reminder information in the number’s separate SMS instructions and enable auto-reply if wanted. Review permission for the corrected recipients and launch once. Any further recipient edit needs another review.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing message detail | The finished text is incomplete. | Correct the recipient data and preview again. |
| Estimated parts changed | Length or encoding changed the estimate. | Review the final text and your telephone rates. |
| Waiting | Local hours or the scheduled date prevent sending now. | Review the recipient’s timing. |
| Locked opt-out | The customer has opted out. | Keep the recipient blocked. |
| Uncertain launch | You do not know whether sending started. | Open the saved campaign before any new send. |
