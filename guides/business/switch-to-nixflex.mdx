## List what must work

Write down the numbers, greetings, languages, opening hours, transfers, booking calendars, texts and follow-up automations used today. Include the fallback when a person does not answer or a calendar cannot save an appointment. Keep the current service’s credentials private.

## Rebuild and test one journey

Create the agent, add reviewed knowledge and connect a test number you control. Recreate each required action through the actual Nixflex settings. A prompt that mentions a calendar does not connect that calendar.

Test a normal enquiry, an unknown answer, a correction and a human handoff. For bookings, check the appointment in the real calendar after the required voice spelling and combined approval. Check any text separately. Record what passed and what needs changing.

## Choose the handover

Agree on a time, a responsible person and the old routing destination. Change the carrier routing only after the test result is acceptable. Watch Call History, booking results and Logs immediately afterwards. If the agreed checks fail, use the recorded routing rollback rather than repeatedly changing unrelated settings.

Do not run the same campaign on both services. Keep old history through its existing export and retention process; moving phone routing does not import old calls into Nixflex.
