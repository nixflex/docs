## Review call activity

1. Open **Overview** after signing in to the intended organisation.
2. Select the period you need: today, seven days, thirty days or ninety days.
3. Narrow the view by agent or call direction when comparing a particular workflow.
4. Review calls, minutes, outcomes and the available conversation measures together.
5. Open **Call History** to inspect a particular call, its transcript, recording availability or booking result.

The recent-call list is a short list of the latest calls. It is not the complete history for the selected period. Use the filters in [Call History](/guides/business/call-history) for an individual investigation.

## Understand the figures

| Figure | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Calls and minutes | Compare the selected activity window. Check the time zone and filters when comparing another screen. |
| Success and outcomes | These describe the recorded call result; they do not prove that a booking exists or an email arrived. |
| Sentiment and response measures | Read them as available conversation measures. A missing value is not a perfect score or a zero. |
| Credit and cost | Owner and Admin can review money information. Use Billing for charges and payment documents. |

For the actual appointment or its changes, open [Booking result](/guides/business/booking-results). For a charge, use [check your charges](/guides/business/billing/check-charges). Retained history is not a complete billing ledger.

## Find help and service status

The sidebar footer links to **Docs** and **System status**, and includes the theme control. System status opens the [public status page](https://www.nixflex.com/status). Its green navigation icon is not a live health indicator; read the status page itself.

Use [Logs](/guides/business/logs) for a request or delivery problem. If the page could not load, choose Retry and wait for a confirmed result rather than treating the empty screen as no activity.
