Use **Call one number** on **Batch calls** for one checked outgoing call.

<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Members are read-only.** An Owner or Admin needs an active agent and an eligible outbound number.
- Review **Their local time** and **Calling hours**, then confirm permission for this agent and number.
- **Waits does not schedule a call.** Call now is available only after a fresh ready check and consent.
- If the outcome is unknown, **do not call again**. Check the saved request’s status or Call History first.

</div>

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Call one number">

Go to **Batch calls** and choose **Call one number**. This starts the single-call form, not a new recipient list.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the person, agent and number">

Enter **Number to call** with its country code. Choose **Agent** and **From number**. The from-number choices must belong to that agent and be eligible for outgoing calls.

</Step>

<Step title="Add a short instruction if needed">

Use **Short instruction** for this call’s purpose, up to 500 characters. Leave it blank to use the agent’s saved instructions. Check that the instructions include the facts the call needs.

</Step>

<Step title="Check local time and permission">

Review **Their local time**, **Calling hours** and the result. If needed, open **Recipient location** and use a country or time zone confirmed by the recipient. Choose **Refresh check** after a stale or unsuccessful check.

Tick **I have permission to call this person using this agent and number**. Editing the form clears that consent, so review and confirm it again.

</Step>

<Step title="Call once and follow the result">

Choose **Call now**. Calling hours and permission are checked again. Follow the live status, use **Refresh status** if needed and choose **Open Call history** for the matching call when its ID is known.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What the status means

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ready now** | The current check permits calling now. | Confirm permission before choosing Call now. |
| **Waits** | Calling is not allowed at this time. | Return during the allowed hours and refresh the check. Nothing is scheduled automatically. |
| **Do-not-contact** | The contact policy does not allow the call. | Read the reason and leave the call unstarted while the restriction applies. |
| **Needs a time zone** | The location needs clarification. | Supply a location confirmed by the recipient and check again. |
| **Not checked / Check expired** | There is no current usable check. | Refresh the check before relying on it. |
| **Call needs checking** | The launch outcome is not confirmed. | Refresh status or check Call History without starting another call. |
| **Call not started** | The request was confirmed refused. | Read the reason and fix it before a new attempt. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does Call accepted mean the person answered?">

No. Accepted, ringing, in progress and completed are different states. Read the live status and the saved call result.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I close or reload the page?">

Reopen **Call one number** to check the saved request. A lost response does not make another call safe. Use **Refresh status** or **Open Call history** until the outcome is known.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When can I make another call?">

**Call another number** becomes available after a confirmed refusal or a confirmed ended call. Do not replace a request whose outcome is still unknown.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why is my sending number missing?">

Check that the number is assigned to the chosen agent, active and enabled for outgoing calls. Review **Phone Numbers** before trying again.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
