## Get help

1. Open **Settings → Help**, choose **Support tickets**, then **New ticket**.
2. Choose **Problem**, **Billing**, **Raise call limit**, **Report abuse** or **Other**. For a higher call limit, enter the number of simultaneous calls you need and what you’ll use them for.
3. Enter a **Subject** and **Message**, then select **Send ticket**. Support aims to reply within 48 hours, including weekends.

In **My tickets**, select a subject to read the conversation. Owners and Admins can see their organisation’s tickets. Members can see only the tickets they opened.

**Waiting on customer** means support is waiting for your reply. Write your message and select **Send reply**. When the issue is resolved, choose **Mark solved**. To reopen a solved ticket, write a message and choose **Reply and reopen**.

If the dashboard says the ticket changed, choose **Refresh ticket** and read the latest conversation before sending your update. If it cannot confirm a submission and shows **Retry same request**, use that button to retry the original change.

## Profile and email preferences

In **Settings → Profile**, use **Edit** beside the account name, then **Save**. Personal profile details and the organisation name are separate; use [Team](/guides/business/team) for organisation management.

Owner and Admin can review **Settings → Email notifications**. Payment receipts and low-balance warnings have their own choices; a low-balance warning needs a saved amount. Auto-recharge failure and empty-balance/service-paused notices stay on. Saving a preference does not prove that a particular email reached an inbox.

## Check the outcome

Use the task’s permissions and prerequisites before making changes. Read the saved result and keep an unavailable or uncertain result distinct from confirmed success. The linked guides explain the relevant recovery steps.
