As Owner, change a joined teammate between Admin and Member when their responsibilities change.

  <div><small>Confirm with</small><strong>Saved role in Team</strong></div>

<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Promoting to Admin gives billing and shared-edit access.** Review the role before selecting it.
- An unconfirmed save needs a fresh check of Team, not an assumption that nothing changed.
- The notice email does not decide access. Check the saved role.
- Changing a role does not rotate a separately shared organisation API key.

</div>

## Before you start

- Sign in as the **Owner**. Admins cannot manage Admin roles.
- Check the intended person's name and email in Team.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Team">

<span id="1-open-team" />

Find the existing person in the members list. A pending invitation is not a joined member; manage it through the invitation actions instead.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the role">

<span id="2-choose-the-role" />

Use the person's role control to select **Admin** or **Member**. Review the [role comparison](/guides/business/team-roles) before promoting someone.

</Step>

<Step title="Wait for the saved result">

<span id="3-wait-for-the-saved-result" />

The role change saves when selected. Wait for the success message and check the displayed role. If the save cannot be confirmed, reopen Team and check the current role before trying again.

</Step>

<Step title="Tell the person what changed">

<span id="4-tell-the-person-what-changed" />

Nixflex sends a notice stating the new role. Email failure does not undo the change, and inbox arrival is not guaranteed. The saved role is the access record.

</Step>

<Step title="Review any separately shared access">

<span id="5-review-any-separately-shared-access" />

A role change does not rotate the organisation API key. If the person was given it separately and should no longer use it, the Owner must rotate it separately and update the intended integrations.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Check the saved result

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role changed in Team | The saved role now controls dashboard access. | Check that it is the intended role for the intended person. |
| Save not confirmed | The outcome is uncertain. | Reopen Team and read the current role before trying again. |
| Notice delayed or failed | The email did not confirm inbox delivery. | Use the saved role as the access record and tell the person directly if needed. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Can I make someone Owner here?">

Use **Make owner** and the [ownership offer process](/guides/business/team-ownership). The other person must accept; selecting Admin is not an ownership transfer.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can an Admin promote a Member?">

No. The Owner controls changes between Member and Admin.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does changing to Member delete their work?">

No. Shared agents, numbers and history remain with the business. The Member can still read shared work and use eligible live listening or takeover.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
