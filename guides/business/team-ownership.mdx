Transfer ownership by sending an offer that the intended teammate accepts with their own login.

<div className="nxf-audience">

<div className="nxf-facts">
  <div><small>Who sends the offer</small><strong>Current Owner</strong></div>
  <div><small>Recipient</small><strong>Joined Admin or Member</strong></div>
  <div><small>Pending offers</small><strong>One at a time</strong></div>
  <div><small>Former Owner</small><strong>Becomes Admin</strong></div>
</div>

<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Sending an offer does not transfer ownership.** The intended recipient must accept an active offer.
- The organisation keeps its balance, billing, API key and shared work.
- Both people must complete any requested recent sign-in and two-factor checks.
- Check both saved roles after acceptance; email arrival is not the confirmation.

</div>

## Before you start

- The current Owner sends the offer to an existing Admin or Member.
- Both people should be ready to confirm a recent sign-in and any enabled two-factor authentication when asked.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Offer ownership">

<span id="1-offer-ownership" />

As Owner, open **Team**, find the intended person and choose **Make owner**. Check their identity and complete the sign-in confirmation. An offer email asks them to review it; sending this email does not transfer ownership.

</Step>

<Step title="Check the waiting state">

<span id="2-check-the-waiting-state" />

Only one outstanding offer is allowed. Team names the person you are waiting for and shows **Cancel offer** on their row. Cancel it if you chose the wrong person or no longer want to transfer.

</Step>

<Step title="Open the personal offer">

<span id="3-open-the-personal-offer" />

The recipient opens the review link or **Team** with their own login. Only they see the **Accept** or **Decline** banner. A Member can use this banner without receiving other team-management permissions. If another email is signed in, use **Sign out and continue**.

</Step>

<Step title="Accept or decline">

<span id="4-accept-or-decline" />

Choose **Accept** to take ownership, then complete any requested recent sign-in and two-factor check. Choose **Decline** to leave ownership unchanged. Opening the email alone does neither. A cancelled, expired or wrong-person sign-in cannot complete the transfer.

</Step>

<Step title="Check both roles">

<span id="5-check-both-roles" />

After confirmed acceptance, the recipient is Owner and the former Owner is Admin. Nixflex sends a notice to each person explaining the change. A delayed or failed email does not reverse it; check Team for the saved roles.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the offer state

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waiting for the recipient | Ownership has not changed. | Check the named person; cancel the offer if it is no longer intended. |
| Acceptance confirmed | The recipient is Owner and the former Owner is Admin. | Read both roles in Team before relying on the new access. |
| Cancelled, declined or expired | That offer cannot complete a transfer. | Keep the current ownership record; make a new deliberate offer only if needed. |
| Sign-in not completed | The requested personal check did not approve acceptance. | Return to Team with the intended login and check the offer state. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does the balance or API key move to a new business?">

No. They stay with the same organisation. Its new Owner controls the key and Admins.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can the previous Owner leave?">

After acceptance they are Admin and can [leave the organisation](/guides/business/team-leave). Before acceptance, they are still Owner and must complete a transfer first.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if sign-in opens a separate window?">

Complete sign-in as the same intended person. Keep Team open. A blocked, cancelled or wrong-account sign-in does not approve the transfer; return and check the offer state.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
