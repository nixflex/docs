Ownership changes only when the intended person accepts an active offer. The organisation keeps its balance, billing, API key and work. The previous Owner becomes an Admin.

## Before you start

- The current Owner sends the offer to an existing Admin or Member.
- Both people should be ready to confirm a recent sign-in and any enabled two-factor authentication when asked.

## Steps

### 1. Offer ownership

As Owner, open **Team**, find the intended person and choose **Make owner**. Check their identity and complete the sign-in confirmation. An offer email asks them to review it; sending this email does not transfer ownership.

### 2. Check the waiting state

Only one outstanding offer is allowed. Team names the person you are waiting for and shows **Cancel offer** on their row. Cancel it if you chose the wrong person or no longer want to transfer.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **team-ownership-waiting** — Team as Owner, Ownership offered waiting state and Cancel offer for one fictional person. Use staging test data.</Note>

### 3. Open the personal offer

The recipient opens the review link or **Team** with their own login. Only they see the **Accept** or **Decline** banner. A Member can use this banner without receiving other team-management permissions. If another email is signed in, use **Sign out and continue**.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **team-ownership-banner** — Team as intended recipient, one personal Accept or Decline banner; Member view if practical. Use staging test data.</Note>

### 4. Accept or decline

Choose **Accept** to take ownership, then complete any requested recent sign-in and two-factor check. Choose **Decline** to leave ownership unchanged. Opening the email alone does neither. A cancelled, expired or wrong-person sign-in cannot complete the transfer.

### 5. Check both roles

After confirmed acceptance, the recipient is Owner and the former Owner is Admin. Nixflex sends a notice to each person explaining the change. A delayed or failed email does not reverse it; check Team for the saved roles.

## Common questions

### Does the balance or API key move to a new business?

No. They stay with the same organisation. Its new Owner controls the key and Admins.

### Can the previous Owner leave?

After acceptance they are Admin and can [leave the organisation](/guides/business/team-leave). Before acceptance, they are still Owner and must complete a transfer first.

### What if sign-in opens a separate window?

Complete sign-in as the same intended person. Keep Team open. A blocked, cancelled or wrong-account sign-in does not approve the transfer; return and check the offer state.
