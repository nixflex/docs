Check your business from your phone using the same dashboard and team login. The layout fits the smaller screen; your role and access stay the same.

## Before you start

- Sign in to the intended organisation.
- Use your phone's main browser for the best experience.

## Steps

### 1. Open the dashboard

Sign in as usual. The compact layout appears automatically on a phone. You do not need to switch to a separate Desktop view or install another app.

### 2. Use the menu

Open the menu to move between pages. Scroll within it to reach more items, then close it to make room for your work. Longer pages and forms still scroll normally.

### 3. Find a call

In **Call History**, tap **Filters** to choose the period, agent or other available filters. Tap **Hide filters** when you want more room for the list. A compact row shows key call details; tap it to open the complete record, including available transcript and recording information.

### 4. Set a comfortable text size

Use your browser's text-size or zoom controls if you prefer larger text. You can also turn the phone sideways. Scroll to the end of a form to find its final controls when the keyboard is open.

### 5. Move out of an in-app browser when prompted

Some apps open links inside their own browser. If Nixflex shows its browser prompt:

| Phone | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| iPhone | Choose **Copy link**, open Safari and paste the link into its address bar. The app's menu may also offer **Open in Safari**. |
| Android | Choose **Open in Chrome**, or copy the link and open it in Chrome yourself. |

You may need to sign in again in the main browser. **Continue anyway** closes the prompt for the current session if you choose to stay inside the app.

<Note>Screenshot placeholder: **phone-dashboard-browser** — In-app browser guidance showing Copy link or Open in Chrome and Continue anyway; use a plain dashboard path with no login tokens.</Note>

## What happens next

Wait for the saved confirmation before repeating an action. Open a call's full details to check its current status.

## Common questions

### Is anything hidden from a compact call row?

The row is a summary. Tap it to read the full details you are allowed to see.

### Can I make the text larger?

Yes. Use your browser's text or zoom controls; the dashboard does not lock browser zoom.

### Do I need to change any settings to get this layout?

No. The phone layout is automatic, including if you previously chose Desktop view.
