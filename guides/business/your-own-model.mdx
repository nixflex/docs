## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login.
- Prepare authorised connection details and an approved test number.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Open the connection">

<span id="1-open-the-connection" />

Open **Settings → Data and privacy**, then **Your own model**. Review the account-wide effect before changing a connection used by several agents.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter authorised details">

<span id="2-enter-authorised-details" />

Enter the details supplied by the person managing your connection. The connection must support the required conversation and action capabilities. Keep all private access details out of instructions, shared notes and screenshots.

</Step>

<Step title="Test and save">

<span id="3-test-and-save" />

Choose **Test & save** and wait for the result. Resolve any refusal before trying again. A successful test checks the connection at that moment.

</Step>

<Step title="Check a real conversation">

<span id="4-check-a-real-conversation" />

Use an approved test call. Check ordinary questions, names, dates and a permitted action. Listen for delays and confirm the action result in its destination.

</Step>

<Step title="Review changes and failures">

<span id="5-review-changes-and-failures" />

If the connection fails later, check the call’s actual result and any fallback. Update changed access details and test again. Disconnect deliberately when you want future work to stop using this connection.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

The saved connection applies where supported. Review actual call results and Billing; a saved test is not a guarantee of service availability or a fixed discount.

## Review the model result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Test passed | The connection worked at that moment. | Test a complete approved conversation. |
| Connection refused | The supplied connection did not pass. | Correct the displayed problem before trying again. |
| Later call failed or fell back | Runtime conditions changed. | Inspect that call and update authorised access details if needed. |
| Disconnected | Future supported work stops using this connection. | Manage the external account and charges separately. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does this replace every part of Nixflex?">

No. Other tasks and fallback paths can still use the standard service.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is a successful test permanent proof?">

No. Access, network conditions and the connected service can change.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does disconnecting close my external account?">

No. Manage that account and its charges separately.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
