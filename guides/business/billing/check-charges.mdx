## Before you start

Use an Owner or Admin login and choose the organisation and reporting period. Members do not see Nixflex money amounts. Decide whether you need usage costs, payments or a statement before exporting.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose the period and time zone">

Open **Billing** and select the period you need. Use the business’s actual reporting dates and displayed time zone. A different zone can put activity on a different day or month.

</Step>

<Step title="Inspect usage and individual work">

Review usage types and open detailed rows where available. Check unresolved costs rather than counting them as zero. Connected calls can be billable without a transcript; actual listening or takeover can affect the recorded charge.

</Step>

<Step title="Review payments separately">

In **Payments**, inspect top-ups and auto-recharges. Use **Filter payments** and the page controls as needed. **Export CSV** exports only the page currently shown. A payment record describes money paid, not every item of usage.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the document you need">

In **Documents**, use **View monthly cost statement** for monthly cost and **Invoice** beside a payment for that payment’s document. Keep the currency and period shown on the document.

</Step>

<Step title="Build a period report">

Choose inclusive **Period from** and **Period to** dates, then **Build report**. Current month, year and financial year run to date. After it completes, review the reported dates and time zone before **Print period statement** or **Export period CSV**. If you cancel the report, no account data is changed.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pending amount | Final cost is not yet established. | Keep it unresolved and review after processing. |
| Payments unavailable | The payment list could not be read. | Refresh; do not treat an unavailable list as no payments. |
| No payment invoices for this month | No payment invoices are listed for that selected month. | Check the intended month and payment history. |
| Report complete | The selected period has a result. | Verify dates, time zone and totals before exporting. |
| Print window blocked | The browser could not open the statement window. | Allow the intended pop-up and try printing again. |
