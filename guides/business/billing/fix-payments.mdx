<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- Do not submit another payment just because the browser did not return to Billing.
- Cancel on the waiting reminder does not reverse a payment made in another tab.
- An auto-recharge card change and enabling auto-recharge are separate actions.
- Never send card details, API keys or private environment contents to support.

## Before you start

Use Owner or Admin access. Have the intended organisation, payment time, amount and non-secret reference available. Keep the existing payment tab open when possible until you understand its result.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Read the current Billing state">

Refresh Billing and inspect the balance, payment list and any waiting or failure message. An unavailable payment list is not evidence that no payment exists.

</Step>

<Step title="Return to an unfinished payment">

If the waiting card is shown, choose **Open payment page again** to revisit that payment. Complete the intended flow there. **Cancel** dismisses the reminder only; it cannot undo a payment already completed in the other tab.

</Step>

<Step title="Handle a decline according to its message">

Review the displayed refusal and the saved payment method. Correct the stated issue with the authorised cardholder before another deliberate attempt. Do not treat a pending or unknown outcome as a confirmed decline.

</Step>

<Step title="Check auto-recharge separately">

Review whether it is enabled, its amount and trigger, the selected card and any applicable cap. A new selected card does not enable auto-recharge. If saving settings is unconfirmed, refresh and read the current configuration before resubmitting.

</Step>

<Step title="Escalate unresolved records">

If the state remains unclear, contact support with the organisation context, time, amount and redacted reference. Describe whether you were adding credit, changing a card or saving recharge settings. Keep secrets and full payment details out of the message.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment still being checked | No final outcome has been confirmed. | Wait and inspect the existing record before another payment. |
| Confirmed decline | The payment was refused. | Follow the refusal and review the authorised payment method. |
| Settings could not be checked | The pre-save check did not establish the current balance or card. | Refresh Billing and review before enabling. |
| Settings could not be confirmed | The save outcome is uncertain. | Read current settings before repeating it. |
| Balance still low | A payment may have failed, remained pending or reached a configured limit. | Review payments and recharge settings; a switch alone does not guarantee credit. |
