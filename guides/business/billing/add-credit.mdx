## Before you start

Sign in as Owner or Admin and confirm the intended organisation. Have permission to use the payment method. Check the current balance and any payment already being processed before starting another top-up.

## Complete the task

<Steps>

<Step title="Choose the top-up amount">

Open **Billing** and use its top-up control. Review the amount and the displayed currency. The minimum top-up is $5; use the current account information for your rates and needs.

</Step>

<Step title="Complete the payment page">

Finish the payment in the opened tab, then return to Billing. If Billing says **Waiting for your payment in the other tab...**, use **Open payment page again** to return to that payment. **Cancel** closes the reminder; it does not reverse a payment made in the other tab.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the recorded payment">

Wait for the credit and payment result to appear. If it is still being checked, inspect the saved payment state before starting another payment. A usage total is a different record from money added.

</Step>

<Step title="Review auto-recharge before enabling it">

Check the selected saved card, recharge amount, trigger and applicable cap. Read any confirmation showing your current balance. If the balance is already at or below the trigger, a payment may be attempted on the next automatic check.

</Step>

<Step title="Save and check the current settings">

Confirm the deliberate change and wait for **Auto-recharge settings saved**. If the outcome is not confirmed, refresh Billing before trying again. Choosing another saved card changes future auto-recharges but does not enable them by itself.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Read the result

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Payment confirmed | Credit can be recorded for the organisation. | Check the balance and corresponding payment record. |
| Waiting for payment | The payment has not yet been confirmed in Billing. | Return to the existing payment page or wait; do not create a duplicate. |
| Settings saved | The current auto-recharge configuration is recorded. | Check the amount, trigger and card. |
| Settings unconfirmed | The save outcome is uncertain. | Refresh Billing and read the settings before another attempt. |

Auto-recharge can fail or reach its configured cap. Keep reviewing low-credit and payment messages; enabling it does not guarantee uninterrupted service.
