Run a voice campaign for a clear task, such as confirming appointments or following up on requested information. A campaign saves its own call purpose. It does not automatically borrow the agent’s incoming-call instructions.

## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login and an active outbound-enabled number.
- Prepare recipients you have permission to contact, with any details needed in the call.

## Steps

### 1. Create the campaign

Open **Outbound calls**, choose **New campaign** and select the agent and sending number. Give the campaign a useful name. Write its full purpose: your business, why you are calling, what to ask and when to finish.

### 2. Add the recipients

Paste the list or upload your file. Review detected numbers, duplicate rows and personal details. Fix missing information used in the call purpose. Editing recipient input clears earlier permission confirmation and any row-specific decisions.

### 3. Review contact hours

Choose the date, days and sending window. The checks use each recipient’s possible local time zones. Add a country or time zone when you know better. A preview is guidance; every recipient is checked again before contact.

### 4. Confirm and launch

Confirm permission for the reviewed list and launch once. Invalid numbers can be skipped only with the relevant option; missing required message details still need correction. Wider hours require permission and a reason, and do not bypass hard limits.

### 5. Track the outcome

Open the saved campaign to follow each recipient. Read waiting and held reasons before acting. If launch has an unknown result, keep the campaign and check it again. **Cancel** stops further dispatch; **Resume** continues eligible unsent work.

## What happens next

Calls proceed when recipients pass the current checks and capacity is available. A scheduled time is not a promise that every phone rings at that moment. Review individual results before planning another campaign.

## Common questions

### Does Cancel hang up live calls?

No. Calls already started may finish. Cancellation is not a hang-up control.

### Does Resume call everyone again?

No. It continues eligible unsent work; it does not resend completed contacts.

### What does Location unknown mean?

The system cannot safely determine contact timing. Correct the recipient’s location details rather than treating the row as ready.

### Does a preview prove legal permission?

No. Keep your own permission records and business contact rules; the preview is an operational check.
