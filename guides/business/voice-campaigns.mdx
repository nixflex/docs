<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Write the campaign’s full purpose.** It does not automatically borrow the agent’s incoming-call instructions.
- Review recipients, local calling hours and permission before launching. A preview sends no calls.
- **Check an uncertain result before trying again.** A missing response does not prove that no call started.
- Only calling one person? Use [Call one number](/guides/business/call-one-number) from the same **Batch calls** page.

</div>

## Set it up

<Steps>

<Step title="Create the campaign">

<span id="1-create-the-campaign" />

Open **Batch calls → New campaign**. Choose an active agent and an outbound-enabled sending number. Give the campaign a useful name and write the business, reason for calling, questions to ask and when to finish. Owners and Admins can launch; Members are read-only.

</Step>

<Step title="Review the recipients">

<span id="2-add-the-recipients" />

Paste the list or upload your file. Check detected numbers, duplicate rows and personal details used in the call purpose. Editing recipient input clears earlier permission confirmation and row-specific decisions.

</Step>

<Step title="Check local calling hours">

<span id="3-review-contact-hours" />

Choose the date, days and calling window. Checks use each recipient’s possible local time zones. Add a confirmed country or time zone where needed. Every recipient is checked again before contact.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm permission and launch once">

<span id="4-confirm-and-launch" />

Confirm permission for the reviewed list. Invalid numbers can be skipped only with the relevant option; missing required details still need fixing. Wider hours need permission and a reason, and do not bypass hard limits.

</Step>

<Step title="Open the campaign to follow results">

<span id="5-track-the-outcome" />

Click its row or press Enter to open the details. Read each recipient’s result and any waiting reason. **Use again** starts a new setup to review; opening the row itself opens the saved campaign.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What the status means

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Status or result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | The campaign has a scheduled start. | Review the start time and recipient checks; scheduling does not guarantee every call can proceed. |
| **Running** | Calls proceed as checks pass and capacity becomes available. | Follow the individual call results before launching more work for the same recipients. |
| **Needs checking** | An outcome is uncertain. | Keep the saved campaign and check its results before launching more work. |
| **Cancelled** | Further calls will not be dispatched; started calls may finish. | Review the final call outcomes separately. |
| **Completed** | Campaign work has finished. | Review individual outcomes. This does not mean everyone answered. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does Resume call everyone again?">

No. It continues eligible unsent work. It does not repeat completed contacts. A scheduled time is not a promise that every phone rings at that moment.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does Location unknown mean?">

Nixflex cannot safely determine contact timing. Correct the recipient’s location rather than treating the row as ready.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I check before removing a campaign?" id="why-is-campaign-deletion-waiting">

Read the current dashboard confirmation and check the saved campaign state. If the outcome is unclear, contact support before repeating a destructive action. Required billing records and customer follow-up have separate purposes.

</Accordion>

<span id="does-a-preview-prove-i-have-permission" style={{ scrollMarginTop: "8rem" }} />

<Accordion title="Does a preview prove I have permission?" id="does-a-preview-prove-legal-permission">

No. Keep your own permission records and business contact rules. The preview is an operational check.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
