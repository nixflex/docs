  <div><small>Export range</small><strong>Up to 12 months</strong></div>
</div>

<div className="nxf-important">

**The important part**

- **Not confirmed does not mean Done.** Read the recorded outcome before repeating an action.
- Activity describes actions; it is not a conversation transcript or an inbox-delivery receipt.
- The CSV includes every matching event in the selected range, with no 500-row export cap.
- Keep the downloaded file private. Customers cannot edit or remove Activity entries.

</div>

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Open Activity">

<span id="1-open-activity" />

Open **Team**, then **Activity**. Each entry describes an action in plain words with the person and result where known. Older records can have generic labels when the original person or item is no longer available.

</Step>

<Step title="Filter the list">

<span id="2-filter-the-list" />

Choose a **Person** and optional **From** and **To** dates. Dates use **UTC**. If the day differs from your local time, adjust the range accordingly. The view refreshes when opened, when the window gets focus and after your own team action.

</Step>

<Step title="Read the result">

<span id="3-read-the-result" />

On a phone, swipe the table sideways if you cannot see every column.

| Result or wording | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Done** | The recorded action completed. | Read the person and action to confirm it is the event you need. |
| **Not allowed** | The action was refused. | Check the person’s role and the action’s permission requirements. |
| **Not confirmed** | Completion could not be established. | Check the affected item before assuming success or repeating the action. |
| Asked to end a call | Ending was requested; completion is not yet confirmed. | Read the call’s current result. |

</Step>

<Step title="Read more or export">

<span id="4-read-more-or-export" />

**Load 50 more** adds another page. For a complete filtered record, choose the CSV download. It streams every matching event in the chosen range, up to **12 months**. There is no 500-row export cap. The **When** column uses a readable UTC date and time.

</Step>

<Step title="Keep the file private">

<span id="5-keep-the-file-private" />

Store the export with the people who need to review it. The export fixes its upper boundary when it starts, so new actions do not move later pages. Download again for newer events. Customers cannot edit or delete Activity entries.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Why does listening show one entry?">

One displayed listening session includes its duration after playback starts. A takeover records actual human activation. Internal access checks are not extra customer actions.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="When does adding knowledge appear?">

The named addition appears after publication succeeds. A draft, failed import or review-only job must not say knowledge was added.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Activity show secrets or conversation text?">

No. It describes actions without showing credentials, message contents or raw item identifiers. It is not a transcript or an email inbox-delivery report.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
