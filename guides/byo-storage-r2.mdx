**The important part**

- A successful connection test does not guarantee every upload.
- Fallback may use standard storage, so this setting does not guarantee one storage region.
- Your bucket may need its own authorised player or download path.
- Disconnecting storage or removing a call does not remove files already in your bucket.

## Before you start

- Use an Owner or Admin login.
- Have authorised bucket details and a person who manages its access and retention.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Open recording storage">

<span id="1-open-recording-storage" />

Open **Settings**, then **Data and privacy**, and find **Recording storage**. This is an account connection, so review the effect on all affected agents before changing it.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the connection details">

<span id="2-enter-the-connection-details" />

Enter the bucket, its region, the appropriate secure endpoint and its access details. Get these from your storage administrator. Never paste private access details into agent instructions or a screenshot.

</Step>

<Step title="Test and save">

<span id="3-test-and-save" />

Choose **Test & save**. Wait for the result. A successful test checks the connection now; it does not guarantee every later upload will succeed.

</Step>

<Step title="Check a test recording">

<span id="4-check-a-test-recording" />

Make an approved test call with recording enabled. Open its history and check the storage result. A recording stored in your bucket is managed through that bucket’s authorised access; it may not have an ordinary dashboard player.

</Step>

<Step title="Manage failures and retention">

<span id="5-manage-failures-and-retention" />

If your bucket rejects an upload, standard Nixflex storage may be attempted. Review the call result and fix the connection. Set your own bucket retention; removing a call or disconnecting storage does not remove files already kept there.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What happens next

New recordings follow the saved connection and actual upload outcome. Keep the storage administrator involved when credentials, access rules or retention change.

## Read the storage outcome

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stored in your bucket | The recording used your configured storage. | Access it through your authorised bucket tools. |
| Fallback used | The chosen bucket upload did not provide the saved result. | Check the standard storage result and fix the bucket connection. |
| No saved recording | Both the intended upload and fallback can fail. | Inspect the call result; do not assume audio exists. |
| Connection removed | Future storage follows the remaining configuration. | Retain or remove existing bucket files under your own policy. |

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does this guarantee one storage region?">

No. Fallback can use standard storage, so this switch alone is not a regional exclusivity guarantee.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are recordings guaranteed to be saved?">

No. Both the chosen bucket and fallback can fail; inspect the actual result.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when I disconnect?">

Future work uses the remaining recording configuration. Existing bucket files stay under your control.

</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
